# Menu Book — Troop Intro (2-3 min)

Give this same short rundown twice: once to patrol leaders, once to the
troop at large. Bullet fragments only — these are speaking notes, not a
script. Glance and talk, don't read it aloud.

## Why we're doing this

- Menu planning used to be scrambled together the week before — now
  it's pre-planned.
- Recipes, shopping lists, allergy tags, cost estimates — all done
  already.
- Two documents: Front-Country (car camping) and Backpacking (trail) —
  pick from them, don't plan from scratch.
- Book and website both — patrol leaders should know both exist.

## How to use it

- One Main per meal slot, plus a Side or Dessert where the meal calls
  for one.
- Check the tag bar first — diet, nut-free, egg-free — before picking a
  recipe, not after.
- Advance Prep (at home) vs. Onsite (at camp) — every recipe splits it
  for you.

## Vegetarian & allergy policy

- Vegetarian base + meat boost, cooked separately, added at serving —
  not two separate meals.
- Nut-free / egg-free tagged on every recipe — always double-check
  actual labels at the store, brands change.

## Nutrition

- "Real Food Check" instead of calorie counting — protein, vegetables,
  real food vs. processed shortcuts.
- Backpacking also tracks weight and calories/protein per serving — a
  real packing constraint on trail.

## Pricing

- Weekend budget target: roughly $25/scout.
- Every recipe has a cost estimate built in — flag it to the committee
  if a weekend's running over.

## Advancement — in priority order

1. **Tenderfoot → Second Class:** req 2e, cook a hot breakfast or lunch.
2. **Scout-rank (not yet Tenderfoot) → Second Class:** pair with a
   Tenderfoot scout cooking for #1 — the assist *is* their Tenderfoot
   requirement, same meal.
3. **Second Class → First Class:** req 2a, plan *and* cook all three
   meals, explain the nutrition using the Real Food Check.
4. **Cooking merit badge** (mention last, address oldest/highest-rank
   scouts first): camp + trail cooking, can't double-count a meal for
   rank advancement too.

## Close with

- Everything is in the book and on the website.
- No new paperwork — lean on senior scouts to walk newer ones through
  it, campout by campout.
