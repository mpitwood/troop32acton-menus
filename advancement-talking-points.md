# Menu Book — 2-3 Minute Advancement Intro

Give this same short rundown twice: once to patrol leaders, once to the
troop at large. Talking points, not a script — glance and talk, don't
read it aloud.

**The pitch:** our senior scouts already know how to cook and plan a
menu — this book doesn't replace that, it just makes it a little easier
and more consistent. Recipes, shopping lists, allergy tags, and cost
estimates are already worked out. No separate forms to fill out — a
senior scout walking a younger one through a tagged recipe *is* the
requirement being met. Patrol leaders: lean on your senior scouts to
walk newer scouts through this informally, campout to campout.

## 1. Tenderfoot scouts working toward Second Class

- Second Class requirement 2e: serve as cook for one hot breakfast or
  lunch on a campout.
- Point them at any recipe tagged **Second Class**. Have a senior scout
  walk them through picking it, planning it, and explaining why it's a
  real meal — that conversation covers the requirement.

## 2. New Scouts working toward Tenderfoot

- Just assist in preparing one meal — any meal in the book works.
- Simplest requirement in the book. Any patrol member cooking that day
  can bring a new scout in to help.

## 3. Second Class scouts working toward First Class

- First Class requirement 2a is bigger: help **plan** a full menu
  (breakfast, lunch, dinner — at least two cooked) *and* serve as cook
  for all three.
- Has to be able to explain how the menu covers real nutrition — every
  recipe's Real Food Check is the talking point for that, no separate
  writeup needed.

## 4. Cooking merit badge (mention last — address the oldest/highest-rank scouts in the room first)

- Usually Star and up.
- Camp cooking and trail cooking are the two methods to cover.
- One rule to flag: a meal used for the merit badge can't double-count
  for rank advancement — don't reuse the same menu for both.

**Close with:** Everything is in the book and on the website — recipes,
tags, the full guide. No extra paperwork. Trust your senior scouts to
walk the rest of the patrol through it, campout by campout.
