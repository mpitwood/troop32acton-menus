Camp Pancakes

Breakfast · Main · Vegetarian · Nut-Free: Yes · Egg-Free: No (swap below) · 2-Burner Stove · Serves 6 · Prep 5 min · Cook 15-20 min

The reliable, crowd-pleasing default — cheap, fast, and everyone can eat the base recipe. Good as a lighter breakfast or paired with a meat side (bacon, sausage links) that isn't tracked as a separate page here.

Ingredients (serves 6, ~3 pancakes each)

  • 3 cups pancake mix (a "just add water/milk" complete mix keeps the ingredient list short)
  • 2 cups milk
  • 2 large eggs (or egg-free swap, see below)
  • 3 tbsp oil or melted butter
  • Butter or oil, for the griddle
  • Syrup

Shopping List

  • Pancake mix, one box (enough for 3 cups)
  • Milk, small carton
  • Eggs (2, or skip if using flax swap)
  • Oil or butter
  • Syrup

Equipment

  • 2-burner stove
  • Large flat griddle or skillet
  • Spatula, mixing bowl, whisk

Instructions

Advance Prep (at home)

  • If using the flax-egg swap, no advance prep needed — it's mixed fresh.
  • Decant the 3 tbsp of oil (or soften the butter) into a small travel container instead of packing a full bottle.
  • Pour syrup into a small squeeze bottle rather than hauling the full jug, unless the troop is already bringing one for other meals.

Onsite (at camp)

  1. Whisk mix, milk, eggs (or flax egg, see below), and oil together until just combined — some lumps are fine, don't overmix.
  2. Heat griddle over medium, lightly oiled.
  3. Pour ~1/4 cup batter per pancake. Flip when bubbles form across the surface and edges look set, about 2 minutes a side.
  4. Keep finished pancakes covered with foil to stay warm while cooking the rest.

Real Food Check

  • Protein: eggs, milk — present but modest, not the anchor of the meal
  • Processed: boxed pancake mix, syrup (added sugar)
  • Pair with a protein-forward side (eggs, sausage) rather than serving pancakes and syrup alone

Allergy & Diet Notes

  • Nut-free: yes, as written.
  • Egg-free swap: replace the 2 eggs with 2 tbsp ground flaxseed mixed with 6 tbsp water, left to sit 5 minutes until gel-like. Pancakes come out slightly denser but cook and taste fine. Double-check the pancake mix box itself doesn't already contain egg powder.
  • Vegetarian: yes, as written (no meat).

Cost Estimate

~$1.95/scout for 6: mix ~$3.50, milk ~$1.20, eggs ~$0.95, oil/butter ~$0.50, syrup ~$5.50. Boston-area pricing — see README.md for sourcing.

Scaling Notes

  • Patrol of 4: 2 cups mix, 1.25 cups milk, 1-2 eggs.
  • Patrol of 8: 4 cups mix, 2.5 cups milk, 3 eggs — cook in more batches rather than crowding the griddle, or use two pans across both burners.

Advancement Tie-In

  • Tenderfoot: assist in preparing this meal.
  • Second Class: counts as the hot breakfast a scout cooks solo.
  • First Class: works as the hot breakfast in a planned menu.
  • Cooking merit badge: satisfies the camp stove cooking method requirement.
