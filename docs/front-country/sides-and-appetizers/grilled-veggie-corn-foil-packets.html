Grilled Veggie & Corn Foil Packets

Any meal · Side · Vegan · Nut-Free: Yes · Egg-Free: Yes · Foil Pack/Coals · Serves 6 · Prep 10 min · Cook 15-20 min

A colorful, genuinely tasty side that cooks on the same coals as whatever foil-packet main the patrol is making — no extra fire, no extra pot, works for every diet in the patrol without modification.

Ingredients (serves 6)

  • 4 ears corn, kernels cut off (or 2 cups canned corn, drained — easier to pack than fresh cobs and needs no cooler space)
  • 2 zucchini, sliced into half-moons
  • 1 bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 red onion, sliced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • Salt and pepper
  • Optional: lime wedges, chopped cilantro, grated parmesan or cotija (skip for vegan)

Shopping List

  • Corn, 4 ears (or 2 cups canned)
  • Zucchini, 2
  • Bell pepper, 1
  • Red onion, 1
  • Olive oil
  • Garlic powder
  • Optional: lime, cilantro, parmesan/cotija
  • Heavy-duty foil

Equipment

  • Fire or coal bed with a cooking grate
  • Heavy-duty aluminum foil

Instructions

Advance Prep (at home)

  • Slice the zucchini, bell pepper, and onion ahead — all hold up fine in a cooler for a day or two. Cut corn off the cob at home too, if using fresh, so it's ready to toss straight into the packets.
  • Decant the olive oil and garlic powder into small travel containers.

Onsite (at camp)

  1. Toss all vegetables with olive oil, garlic powder, salt, and pepper.
  2. Divide evenly across 2-3 double-layered foil packets. Seal tightly.
  3. Place on a grate over medium-hot coals. Cook 15-20 minutes, flipping once, until vegetables are tender.
  4. Open carefully. Finish with a squeeze of lime and cheese if using.

Real Food Check

  • Protein: minimal — this is a vegetable side, not a protein source
  • Vegetables: corn, zucchini, bell pepper, red onion
  • Processed: none

Allergy & Diet Notes

  • Nut-free: yes, as written.
  • Egg-free: yes.
  • Vegan: yes, as written (skip the cheese topping).

Cost Estimate

~$1.65/scout for 6: corn ~$4, zucchini ~$2.10, pepper ~$1.75, onion ~$1.30, oil/seasoning ~$0.75. Boston-area pricing — see README.md for sourcing.

Scaling Notes

  • Patrol of 4: 3 ears corn, 1.5 zucchini.
  • Patrol of 8: 6-8 ears corn, 3 zucchini — split across 4-5 packets so each cooks evenly.

Advancement Tie-In

  • Tenderfoot: assist in preparing this meal.
  • Cooking merit badge: satisfies the foil-pack cooking method requirement — an easy way to check that box without a whole separate meal.
