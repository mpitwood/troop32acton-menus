Campfire Kettle Popcorn

Any meal · Snack · Vegan · Nut-Free: Yes · Egg-Free: Yes · 2-Burner Stove · Serves 6 · Prep 5 min · Cook 5-8 min

Cheapest snack in the whole repo, and shaking the pot over the burner is part of the fun — good for a campfire program night.

Ingredients (serves 6)

  • 1/2 cup popcorn kernels
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • Salt (or 2 tbsp sugar for a sweet kettle-corn version)

Shopping List

  • Popcorn kernels, 1 bag (1/2 cup needed)
  • Oil
  • Salt (or sugar)

Equipment

  • 2-burner stove
  • Large pot with a lid

Instructions

Advance Prep (at home)

  • Decant the oil into a small travel container — only 3 tbsp is needed.
  • Portion the kernels into a small bag if the bulk bag is large; only 1/2 cup is needed per batch.

Onsite (at camp)

  1. Heat oil in the large pot over medium-high heat with 3 test kernels dropped in.
  2. Once the test kernels pop, add the remaining kernels (and sugar, if making kettle corn). Cover.
  3. Shake the pot frequently over the burner until popping slows to 2-3 seconds between pops, about 3-5 minutes.
  4. Remove from heat immediately — popcorn burns fast once popping slows. Season with salt.

Real Food Check

  • Protein: minimal
  • Whole food: popcorn kernels are a whole grain in its actual whole form, not milled/refined
  • Processed: none

Allergy & Diet Notes

  • Nut-free: yes.
  • Egg-free: yes.
  • Vegan: yes.

Cost Estimate

~$0.80/scout for 6: kernels ~$3.50, oil ~$0.75, salt negligible. Boston-area pricing — see README.md for sourcing.

Scaling Notes

  • Patrol of 4: 1/3 cup kernels.
  • Patrol of 8: pop in two batches rather than doubling kernels in one pot — overcrowded kernels don't pop evenly and scorch on the bottom.

Advancement Tie-In

  • Cooking merit badge: satisfies the camp stove method and the snack requirement for camp cooking.
