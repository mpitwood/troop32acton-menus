Nut-Free Snack Basket

Any meal · Snack · Vegetarian (dairy-free option noted) · Nut-Free: Yes · Egg-Free: Yes · No-Cook · Serves 6 · Prep 10 min · Cook: none

Two no-cook snack options, both built to be safe by default for a scout with a nut allergy rather than requiring a special substitution — a crunchy trail mix and a fruit-and-cheese plate. Lay both out and let the patrol graze between activities.

Ingredients (serves 6)

Sunflower seed trail mix:

  • 2 cups roasted, salted sunflower seeds (shelled)
  • 1 cup dried cranberries or raisins
  • 1 cup pretzel pieces
  • 1 cup nut-free chocolate chips or M&M's (M&M's are made in a facility that also processes peanuts — check current packaging if that matters for the specific allergy)

Fruit & cheese plate:

  • 2 apples, sliced
  • 1 lb grapes
  • 8 oz cheese cubes or string cheese
  • 1 sleeve crackers (check label for a nut-free brand)

Shopping List

  • Sunflower seeds, roasted and salted, 2 cups
  • Dried cranberries or raisins, 1 cup
  • Pretzels, 1 cup
  • Nut-free chocolate chips, 1 cup
  • Apples, 2
  • Grapes, 1 lb
  • Cheese (cubes or string cheese), 8 oz
  • Crackers, 1 sleeve (nut-free brand)

Equipment

  • Mixing bowl (for trail mix) or just combine in a bag
  • Cutting board and knife (for apples)

Instructions

Advance Prep (at home)

  • Mix the trail mix at home and portion into a large bag or several small ones for hikes — no assembly needed at camp.
  • Cube the cheese ahead if using block cheese instead of string cheese.

Onsite (at camp)

  1. Trail mix: if not already mixed at home, combine sunflower seeds, dried fruit, pretzels, and chocolate in a large bowl or gallon bag. Shake to mix.
  2. Fruit & cheese plate: slice apples just before serving (toss with a little lemon juice or water if prepping more than 30 minutes ahead, to slow browning). Arrange with grapes, cheese, and crackers.

Real Food Check

  • Protein: sunflower seeds, cheese
  • Fruit: apples, grapes, dried cranberries/raisins
  • Processed: trail mix leans processed (chocolate/candy, pretzels); the fruit-and-cheese plate is straightforwardly whole-food

Allergy & Diet Notes

  • Nut-free: built to be nut-free from the ingredient list itself, not by substitution — read chocolate/candy and cracker labels for shared- facility warnings regardless, since this changes by brand and by year.
  • Egg-free: yes.
  • Dairy-free option: skip the cheese, add extra fruit or pretzels.

Cost Estimate

~$5.50/scout for 6: sunflower seeds ~$5.50, dried fruit ~$4, pretzels ~$2.75, chocolate ~$4, apples ~$2.75, grapes ~$4.50, cheese ~$5.50, crackers ~$4. Boston-area pricing — see README.md for sourcing.

Scaling Notes

  • Patrol of 4: roughly 2/3 of every quantity.
  • Patrol of 8: roughly 1.3x every quantity — trail mix scales easily since it's just proportional mixing.

Advancement Tie-In

  • Cooking merit badge: satisfies the snack requirement for camp cooking (no-cook still counts — the requirement is about planning and serving, not necessarily heat).
