Ants on a Log (Sunflower Seed Butter)

Any meal · Snack · Vegan · Nut-Free: Yes (sunflower seed butter, check brand) · Egg-Free: Yes · No-Cook · Serves 6 · Prep 10 min · Cook: none

The classic camp snack, made safe by default with sunflower seed butter instead of peanut butter — nobody has to ask "does this have nuts," it simply doesn't.

Ingredients (serves 6, ~2 logs each)

  • 12 celery stalks, cut into 4" logs
  • 1 (16 oz) jar sunflower seed butter (nut-free peanut butter alternative)
  • 1 cup raisins

Shopping List

  • Celery, 1 bunch (12 logs needed)
  • Sunflower seed butter, 1 jar (16 oz)
  • Raisins, 1 cup

Equipment

  • Cutting board and knife
  • Butter knife or small spoon, for spreading

Instructions

Advance Prep (at home)

  • Cut the celery into logs ahead and cooler-pack — holds up fine for a day or two.
  • No need to decant the sunflower seed butter jar itself; it's already a reasonably sized container for the trip.

Onsite (at camp)

  1. Fill each celery log with sunflower seed butter.
  2. Top with a line of raisins ("ants").

Real Food Check

  • Protein: sunflower seed butter
  • Vegetables/fruit: celery, raisins
  • Processed: none

Allergy & Diet Notes

  • Nut-free: sunflower seed butter is the standard nut-free substitute for peanut butter, but always check the specific jar — some brands are processed in facilities that also handle tree nuts.
  • Egg-free: yes.
  • Vegan: yes.

Cost Estimate

~$2.10/scout for 6: celery ~$2.80, sunflower seed butter ~$7, raisins ~$2.80. Boston-area pricing — see README.md for sourcing.

Scaling Notes

  • Patrol of 4: 8 celery logs.
  • Patrol of 8: 16 celery logs — scales linearly, no batching needed.

Advancement Tie-In

  • Cooking merit badge: satisfies the snack requirement for camp cooking — no-cook still counts.
