Veggie Dippers with Hummus

Any meal · Snack · Vegan · Nut-Free: Check hummus label · Egg-Free: Yes · No-Cook · Serves 6 · Prep 10 min · Cook: none

Simple, fast, and it's the vegetable-heaviest snack in the repo — a good pick on a day when the meals already leaned carb- and protein-heavy.

Ingredients (serves 6)

  • 2 cups baby carrots
  • 2 bell peppers, sliced into strips
  • 2 cucumbers, sliced into sticks
  • 2 (10 oz) tubs hummus

Shopping List

  • Baby carrots, 2 cups
  • Bell peppers, 2
  • Cucumbers, 2
  • Hummus, 2 tubs (10 oz each)

Equipment

  • Cutting board and knife

Instructions

Advance Prep (at home)

  • Slice the peppers and cucumbers into dipping-size pieces ahead — both hold up fine in a cooler for a day or two. Baby carrots are already ready to go.

Onsite (at camp)

  1. If not already sliced, cut the peppers and cucumbers into dipping-size pieces.
  2. Serve with hummus for dipping.

Real Food Check

  • Protein: hummus (chickpeas, tahini)
  • Vegetables: carrots, bell pepper, cucumber
  • Processed: none

Allergy & Diet Notes

  • Nut-free: most hummus is nut-free, but check the label — some brands add tree nuts or are processed on shared lines.
  • Egg-free: yes.
  • Vegan: yes.

Cost Estimate

~$3.00/scout for 6: carrots ~$2.80, peppers ~$3.50, cucumbers ~$2.70, hummus ~$9 (2 tubs). Boston-area pricing — see README.md for sourcing.

Scaling Notes

  • Patrol of 4: 1.3 cups carrots, 1.3 peppers, 1.3 cucumbers, 1.3 tubs hummus.
  • Patrol of 8: 4 cups carrots, 4 peppers, 4 cucumbers, 3 tubs hummus.

Advancement Tie-In

  • Cooking merit badge: satisfies the snack requirement for camp cooking.
