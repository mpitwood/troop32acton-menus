Foil Packet Fajitas — Vegetarian Base + Chicken/Steak Boost

Dinner · Main · Vegetarian base · Nut-Free: Yes · Egg-Free: Yes · Foil Pack/Coals · Serves 6 (base) + boost scaled to meat-eaters · Prep 15 min · Cook 12-15 min

Foil packets are naturally one-per-person, which makes this the easiest meal in the repo to serve a mixed patrol: everyone gets the same portobello-and-black-bean fajita filling, and meat is added only to the packets of scouts who want it, before sealing.

Ingredients

Base filling (serves 6, fills 6 packets):

  • 4-5 large portobello mushroom caps, gills scraped, sliced thick
  • 1 (15 oz) can black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 3 bell peppers, sliced
  • 1 large onion, sliced
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 2 tbsp fajita seasoning (or 1 packet)
  • 12 flour tortillas
  • 1.5 cups shredded cheese (use a dairy-free shred for vegan)
  • Optional: sour cream, salsa, lime wedges

Meat boost (sized to meat-eating scouts):

  • 1 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs, thinly sliced (or steak, pricier)
  • 1 tbsp oil, 1 tbsp fajita seasoning (matching the base)

Shopping List

  • Portobello mushroom caps, 4-5 large
  • Black beans, 1 can (15 oz)
  • Bell peppers, 3
  • Onion, 1 large
  • Oil, fajita seasoning
  • Flour tortillas, 12
  • Shredded cheese, 1.5 cups
  • Chicken thighs (or steak) — buy based on headcount of meat-eaters
  • Optional: sour cream, salsa, lime
  • Heavy-duty foil

Equipment

  • Fire or coal bed with a cooking grate
  • Heavy-duty aluminum foil (double-layer each packet)
  • Tongs

Instructions

Advance Prep (at home)

  • Decant the oil and fajita seasoning into small travel containers — only a few tablespoons of each are needed for the whole meal.
  • Slice the peppers and onion ahead; they hold up fine in a cooler for a day or two. Slice the mushrooms closer to cook time so they don't get slimy.

Onsite (at camp)

  1. Toss the sliced portobellos, black beans, peppers, and onion with oil and fajita seasoning — this is the shared base filling for every packet.
  2. Separately, toss the sliced chicken (or steak) with its own oil and seasoning in its own bowl.
  3. Build the vegetarian packet(s) first: base filling only, into a double-layered foil packet, sealed. Set aside.
  4. Build the remaining packets for meat-eating scouts: base filling, then add the seasoned meat on top, seal.
  5. Cook all packets together on a grate over medium-hot coals, 12-15 minutes, flipping once. Chicken needs to reach full doneness; check the thickest piece before serving.
  6. Open carefully — hot steam escapes. Serve the filling in warmed tortillas with cheese and toppings.

Real Food Check

  • Protein (base): portobello mushrooms, black beans; + chicken or steak (boost)
  • Vegetables: bell pepper, onion
  • Processed: none

Allergy & Diet Notes

  • Nut-free: yes.
  • Egg-free: yes.
  • Vegan: base is vegan with a dairy-free cheese shred or no cheese.
  • Assembling the vegetarian packet(s) first, before the raw meat bowl is even opened, avoids any cross-contact — not just separate seasoning bowls but a genuinely separate prep step.

Cost Estimate

Base: ~$4.10/scout for 6 (mushrooms ~$7.50, black beans ~$1.50, peppers ~$5.25, onion ~$1.30, oil/seasoning ~$1.75, tortillas ~$4.40, cheese ~$2.85). Meat boost: ~$1.45 per meat-eating scout with chicken (1 lb ~$5.75, split ~4 ways) — steak runs closer to ~$3.25/scout. Boston-area pricing — see README.md for sourcing.

Scaling Notes

  • Patrol of 4: base at 2/3 quantities, still makes one packet per scout; chicken sized to however many of the 4 eat meat.
  • Patrol of 8: base at 1.3x quantities across 8 packets; chicken sized per actual meat-eater headcount, not patrol size.

Advancement Tie-In

  • Tenderfoot: assist in preparing this meal.
  • Second Class: counts as a hot meal a scout cooks solo.
  • First Class: works as the dinner slot in a planned menu.
