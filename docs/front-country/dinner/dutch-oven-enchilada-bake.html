Dutch Oven Enchilada Bake — Vegetarian Base + Chicken Boost

Dinner · Main · Vegetarian base · Nut-Free: Yes · Egg-Free: Yes · Dutch Oven · Serves 6 (base) + boost scaled to meat-eaters · Prep 15 min · Cook 30-35 min

A layered casserole-style enchilada bake — torn tortillas instead of individually rolled ones, so it's one shared dish, not thirty small ones. Chicken is warmed separately and spooned onto individual portions.

Ingredients

Base (serves 6):

  • 12 corn or flour tortillas, torn or quartered
  • 2 (10 oz) cans red or green enchilada sauce
  • 1 (15 oz) can black beans, drained
  • 1 cup corn, canned and drained (shelf-stable, no cooler space needed)
  • 1/2 onion, diced
  • 2.5 cups shredded Mexican cheese blend
  • Optional: sliced olives, cilantro, sour cream, for serving

Meat boost (sized to meat-eating scouts):

  • 2 cups cooked, shredded chicken (rotisserie or canned, covers up to 5-6 meat-eating scouts)
  • A splash of enchilada sauce, for warming the chicken

Shopping List

  • Corn or flour tortillas, 12
  • Enchilada sauce, 2 cans (10 oz)
  • Black beans, 1 can (15 oz)
  • Corn, 1 can
  • Onion, 1/2
  • Shredded Mexican cheese blend, 2.5 cups
  • Optional: olives, cilantro, sour cream
  • Cooked shredded chicken — buy based on headcount of meat-eaters

Equipment

  • 12" dutch oven + lid
  • Charcoal chimney, ~24-26 coals
  • Small pot (for warming chicken)
  • Lid lifter, leather gloves

Instructions

Advance Prep (at home)

  • Dice the onion ahead and cooler-pack — holds up fine for a day or two.
  • Canned chicken needs no cooler space at all; if using rotisserie chicken instead, shred it at home and cooler-pack.

Onsite (at camp)

  1. Light ~24-26 coals. Oil the dutch oven.
  2. Layer: a splash of enchilada sauce on the bottom, then torn tortillas, black beans, corn, onion, and cheese. Repeat the layers 2-3 times, finishing with cheese on top.
  3. Cover. Set on 8 coals underneath, 16-17 coals on the lid. Bake 30-35 minutes, rotating the oven and lid a quarter turn every 10 minutes, until bubbly and the cheese is melted through.
  4. While it bakes, warm the shredded chicken in a small pot with a splash of enchilada sauce on a camp stove burner. Keep it in its own pot.
  5. To serve: scoop portions from the dutch oven first — this is the vegetarian-safe dish. Meat-eating scouts top their own portion with warmed chicken from its own pot.

Real Food Check

  • Protein (base): black beans, cheese; + chicken (boost)
  • Vegetables: corn, onion
  • Processed: canned enchilada sauce — a minimally processed pantry item, not ultra-processed; no need to make it from scratch

Allergy & Diet Notes

  • Nut-free: yes.
  • Egg-free: yes.

Cost Estimate

Base: ~$2.85/scout for 6 (tortillas ~$4, enchilada sauce ~$4.70, beans ~$1.50, corn ~$1.50, onion ~$0.65, cheese ~$4.70). Chicken boost: ~$1.25 per meat-eating scout (2 cups canned/rotisserie chicken ~$7, split ~5-6 ways). Boston-area pricing — see README.md for sourcing.

Scaling Notes

  • Patrol of 4: 8 tortillas, scale sauce/beans/cheese to 2/3; chicken sized to however many of the 4 eat meat.
  • Patrol of 8: 16 tortillas, 1.3x sauce/beans/cheese, use a 14" oven if available; chicken sized per actual meat-eater headcount.

Advancement Tie-In

  • Tenderfoot: assist in preparing this meal.
  • Second Class: counts as a hot meal a scout cooks solo.
  • First Class: works as the dinner slot in a planned menu.
  • Cooking merit badge: satisfies the dutch oven cooking method requirement.
