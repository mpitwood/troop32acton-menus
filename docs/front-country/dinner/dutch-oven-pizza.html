Dutch Oven Southwest Pizza — Vegetarian Base + Pepperoni Boost

Dinner · Main · Vegetarian base · Nut-Free: Yes · Egg-Free: Check naan label · Dutch Oven · Serves 6 (base) + boost scaled to meat-eaters · Prep 15 min · Cook 20-25 min (in batches)

Personal-size naan pizzas, same Southwest black bean and veggie topping on every round — pepperoni goes on individual rounds only, for scouts who want it, added right before that round goes in the oven.

Ingredients

Base topping (serves 6, tops 6 rounds):

  • 6 naan or flatbread rounds
  • 1.5 cups pizza sauce
  • 1 (15 oz) can black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 cup corn, canned and drained (shelf-stable, no cooler space needed — fresh or frozen-then-thawed also work if used the day of purchase)
  • 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1/2 bell pepper, diced
  • 2 cups shredded pepper jack or cheddar
  • Fresh cilantro, chopped (optional)
  • Olive oil, cornmeal or flour

Meat boost (sized to meat-eating scouts):

  • 4 oz sliced pepperoni (covers up to 4-5 rounds)

Shopping List

  • Naan or flatbread rounds, 6
  • Pizza sauce, 1.5 cups
  • Black beans, 1 can (15 oz)
  • Corn, 1 can
  • Red onion, 1/2
  • Bell pepper, 1/2
  • Shredded pepper jack or cheddar, 2 cups
  • Pepperoni — buy based on headcount of meat-eaters
  • Optional: fresh cilantro
  • Olive oil, cornmeal or flour

Equipment

  • 12" dutch oven + lid
  • Charcoal chimney, ~20-24 coals
  • Lid lifter, leather gloves

Instructions

Advance Prep (at home)

  • Decant the olive oil and cornmeal/flour into small containers — only a couple tablespoons of each are needed.
  • Slice the onion and dice the pepper ahead; both hold up fine in a cooler for a day or two.
  • Pour the pizza sauce into a smaller squeeze bottle if the store jar is large — easier to portion onto 6 rounds at camp.

Onsite (at camp)

  1. Light ~20-24 coals.
  2. Lightly oil the dutch oven bottom and dust with cornmeal or flour.
  3. Top each naan round with sauce, black beans, corn, red onion, bell pepper, and cheese — this is the same base for every round.
  4. Before baking, add pepperoni only to the rounds for scouts who want meat. Build and bake the vegetarian rounds in the first batch so the vegetarian scout isn't waiting on a later batch.
  5. Cover. Set on 6-8 coals underneath, 14-16 coals on the lid. Bake 8-10 minutes per batch of 2, checking at 6 minutes, until cheese is melted and edges are crisp.
  6. Top with cilantro if using. Keep finished pizzas warm under foil between batches.

Real Food Check

  • Protein (base): black beans, cheese; + pepperoni (boost)
  • Vegetables: corn, red onion, bell pepper
  • Processed: naan/flatbread is a refined grain, doing the normal job of a pizza crust; pepperoni boost is a processed meat, optional

Allergy & Diet Notes

  • Nut-free: yes.
  • Egg-free: most naan is egg-free — check the specific product label.

Cost Estimate

Base: ~$3.15/scout for 6 (naan/flatbread ~$5.40, sauce ~$3, black beans ~$1.50, corn ~$1.50, onion/pepper ~$1.55, cheese ~$3.75, cilantro ~$1.50, oil/cornmeal ~$0.75). Pepperoni boost: ~$0.75 per meat-eating scout (4 oz pepperoni ~$3.75, split ~5 ways). Boston-area pricing — see README.md for sourcing.

Scaling Notes

  • Patrol of 4: 4 naan rounds; pepperoni sized to however many of the 4 eat meat.
  • Patrol of 8: 8 naan rounds, batch-cook regardless of patrol size; pepperoni sized per actual meat-eater headcount.

Advancement Tie-In

  • Tenderfoot: assist in preparing this meal.
  • Second Class: counts as a hot meal a scout cooks solo.
  • First Class: a good example of a vegetarian base being the default, with meat as an add-on rather than a parallel dish.
  • Cooking merit badge: satisfies the dutch oven cooking method requirement.
