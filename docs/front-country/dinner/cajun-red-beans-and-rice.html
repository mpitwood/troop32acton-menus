One-Pot Cajun Red Beans & Rice — Vegetarian Base + Andouille Boost

Dinner · Main · Vegetarian base · Nut-Free: Yes · Egg-Free: Yes · 2-Burner Stove · Serves 6 (base) + boost scaled to meat-eaters · Prep 10 min · Cook 25-30 min

Rice and beans cooked together in one pot with the Cajun "holy trinity" (onion, celery, bell pepper) — filling and cheap on its own, with sliced andouille sausage browned separately for meat-eating scouts to stir in.

Ingredients

Base (serves 6):

  • 2 cups long-grain white rice
  • 4 cups water or broth
  • 2 (15 oz) cans red kidney beans, drained (mash about half for creaminess)
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 bell pepper, diced
  • 2 celery stalks, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1-2 tbsp Cajun seasoning
  • 1 bay leaf
  • Oil, salt, hot sauce (to taste)

Meat boost (sized to meat-eating scouts):

  • 3/4-1 lb andouille or smoked sausage, sliced into coins (covers up to 5-6 meat-eating scouts)

Shopping List

  • Long-grain white rice, 2 cups
  • Kidney beans, 2 cans (15 oz)
  • Onion, 1
  • Bell pepper, 1
  • Celery, 2 stalks
  • Garlic
  • Cajun seasoning
  • Bay leaf
  • Andouille or smoked sausage — buy based on headcount of meat-eaters

Equipment

  • 2-burner stove
  • Large pot with lid (base), small skillet (sausage)
  • Wooden spoon

Instructions

Advance Prep (at home)

  • Dice the onion, bell pepper, and celery (the "holy trinity") ahead — all hold up fine in a cooler for a day or two.
  • Decant the Cajun seasoning into a small container and pack just 1-2 bay leaves loose in a small bag rather than the whole jar/box.
  • Decant any hot sauce you're bringing just for this meal into a small travel bottle.

Onsite (at camp)

  1. In the large pot, heat oil, sauté onion, bell pepper, celery, and garlic 5 minutes.
  2. Add rice, stir to coat. Add water/broth, kidney beans, Cajun seasoning, and bay leaf.
  3. Bring to a boil, reduce heat, cover, and simmer 18-20 minutes until the rice is tender and the liquid is absorbed. Remove the bay leaf.
  4. Meanwhile, brown the sliced sausage in a separate skillet on the other burner. Keep it in its own bowl.
  5. To serve: portion the rice and beans into bowls first — this is the vegetarian-safe dish. Meat-eating scouts stir sausage from its own bowl into their own bowl.

Real Food Check

  • Protein (base): kidney beans; + andouille sausage (boost)
  • Vegetables: onion, bell pepper, celery
  • Processed: none in the base; andouille boost is a processed meat, not required for the dish to stand on its own

Allergy & Diet Notes

  • Nut-free: yes.
  • Egg-free: yes.

Cost Estimate

Base: $2.45/scout for 6 (rice ~$2.65, beans ~$3, onion/pepper/celery ~$6.30, garlic ~$1, seasoning ~$1, broth/oil ~$0.75). Andouille boost: ~$0.95 per meat-eating scout (0.875 lb ~$5.75, split ~6 ways). Boston-area pricing — see README.md for sourcing.

Scaling Notes

  • Patrol of 4: 1.3 cups rice, scale beans/veggies proportionally; sausage sized to however many of the 4 eat meat.
  • Patrol of 8: 2.7 cups rice — use the largest pot available, rice needs room to absorb liquid evenly; sausage sized per actual meat-eater headcount.

Advancement Tie-In

  • Tenderfoot: assist in preparing this meal.
  • Second Class: counts as a hot meal a scout cooks solo.
  • First Class: works as the dinner slot in a planned menu.
  • Cooking merit badge: satisfies the camp stove cooking method requirement.
