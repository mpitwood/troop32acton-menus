Loaded Baked Potato Bar — Vegetarian Base + Bacon Boost

Lunch · Main · Vegetarian base · Nut-Free: Yes · Egg-Free: Yes · Foil Pack/Coals · Serves 6 (base) + boost scaled to meat-eaters · Prep 10 min · Cook 45-60 min

Foil-wrapped potatoes baked right in the coals, split open and loaded up buffet-style. Good for a morning where the patrol is out on an activity — start the potatoes in the coals before you leave, they hold heat well.

Ingredients

Base (serves 6):

  • 6 russet potatoes
  • Oil, salt
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1/2 cup sour cream
  • 2 green onions, sliced
  • 1 (15 oz) can black beans, drained (or steamed broccoli), for extra protein/fiber

Meat boost (sized to meat-eating scouts):

  • 6 slices bacon, cooked and crumbled (covers up to 5-6 meat-eating scouts)

Shopping List

  • Russet potatoes, 6
  • Oil, salt
  • Shredded cheddar cheese, 1 cup
  • Sour cream, 1/2 cup
  • Green onions
  • Black beans, 1 can (15 oz) — or broccoli
  • Bacon — buy based on headcount of meat-eaters
  • Heavy-duty foil

Equipment

  • Fire or coal bed
  • Heavy-duty aluminum foil
  • Tongs, a long skewer or knife (to test doneness)

Instructions

Advance Prep (at home)

  • Decant the oil and salt into small travel containers — only a couple tablespoons of oil and a pinch of salt per potato is needed.
  • Slice the green onions ahead and cooler-pack — they hold up fine for a day or two.

Onsite (at camp)

  1. Rub each potato with oil, sprinkle with salt, wrap individually in foil.
  2. Nestle into the coals (or set on a grate close above them). Cook 45-60 minutes, turning every 15 minutes, until a skewer slides through easily.
  3. While potatoes cook, brown the bacon in a small skillet on a camp stove, drain, keep in its own bowl.
  4. Split each potato open, fluff the inside with a fork. Lay out cheese, sour cream, green onion, and black beans as a topping bar.
  5. To serve: the vegetarian scout builds their potato first from the shared toppings. Meat-eating scouts add bacon from its own bowl to their own potato.

Real Food Check

  • Protein (base): cheese, sour cream, black beans; + bacon (boost)
  • Vegetables: potato, green onion
  • Processed: none in the base; bacon boost is a processed meat

Allergy & Diet Notes

  • Nut-free: yes.
  • Egg-free: yes.

Cost Estimate

Base: ~$2.30/scout for 6 (potatoes ~$6, cheese ~$1.90, sour cream ~$2, green onion ~$1.75, black beans ~$1.50, oil/salt ~$0.75). Bacon boost: ~$0.70 per meat-eating scout (6 slices ~$3.75, split ~5-6 ways). Boston-area pricing — see README.md for sourcing.

Scaling Notes

  • Patrol of 4: 4 potatoes; bacon sized to however many of the 4 eat meat.
  • Patrol of 8: 8 potatoes — start them earlier or bank more coals, a crowded coal bed cooks slower per potato.

Advancement Tie-In

  • Tenderfoot: assist in preparing this meal.
  • Second Class: counts as a hot meal a scout cooks solo.
  • First Class: works as the lunch slot in a planned menu.
  • Cooking merit badge: satisfies the foil-pack cooking method requirement.
