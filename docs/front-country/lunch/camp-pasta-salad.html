Camp Pasta Salad

Lunch · Main · Vegetarian base, meat add-in optional · Nut-Free: Yes · Egg-Free: Yes · 2-Burner Stove · Serves 6 · Prep 15 min (plus cooling) · Cook 10 min

A cold lunch for a hot, busy day — cook the pasta once in the morning, let it chill in the cooler, serve at lunch with no reheating needed. Chickpeas carry the protein so it's filling without meat.

Ingredients (serves 6)

  • 1 lb rotini or bowtie pasta
  • 1 cup Italian dressing (oil-and-vinegar based, not mayo — travels and keeps better in a cooler)
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1 cucumber, diced
  • 1/2 red onion, diced
  • 1 cup mozzarella pearls or diced cheese
  • 1 (15 oz) can chickpeas, drained
  • Italian seasoning or fresh basil, salt, pepper
  • Optional: 6 oz sliced pepperoni or salami, for meat-eating scouts

Shopping List

  • Rotini or bowtie pasta, 1 lb
  • Italian dressing, 1 cup
  • Cherry tomatoes, 1 cup
  • Cucumber, 1
  • Red onion, 1/2
  • Mozzarella pearls or diced cheese, 1 cup
  • Chickpeas, 1 can (15 oz)
  • Italian seasoning
  • Optional: pepperoni or salami

Equipment

  • 2-burner stove
  • Large pot, colander
  • Large mixing bowl

Instructions

Advance Prep (at home)

  • Pour the 1 cup of Italian dressing needed into a smaller travel bottle rather than packing the full store bottle.
  • Decant Italian seasoning into a small container.
  • Dice the cucumber and red onion ahead — both hold up fine for a day or two in the cooler; halve the cherry tomatoes on site so they don't turn watery.

Onsite (at camp)

  1. Boil pasta per package instructions. Drain and rinse under cool water (or spread on a tray in the cooler) to stop the cooking and cool it down for a cold salad.
  2. Toss the cooled pasta with dressing, tomatoes, cucumber, onion, cheese, chickpeas, and seasoning. Chill in the cooler 20-30 minutes if time allows — it's fine to eat right away too, just less cold.
  3. Serve. Meat-eating scouts add pepperoni or salami to their own bowl.

Real Food Check

  • Protein: chickpeas, mozzarella
  • Vegetables: cherry tomatoes, cucumber, red onion
  • Processed: bottled dressing — swap in a homemade oil-and-vinegar mix for a fully whole-food version

Allergy & Diet Notes

  • Nut-free: yes.
  • Egg-free: yes (using an oil-and-vinegar dressing, not a mayo-based one, keeps this reliably egg-free — mayo contains egg).

Cost Estimate

Base: ~$2.80/scout for 6 (pasta ~$2.15, dressing ~$4, tomatoes ~$1.70, cucumber ~$1.35, onion ~$0.65, cheese ~$4.75, chickpeas ~$1.50). Meat add-in: ~$0.95 per meat-eating scout (6 oz pepperoni/salami ~$5.60, split ~6 ways). Boston-area pricing — see README.md for sourcing.

Scaling Notes

  • Patrol of 4: 2/3 lb pasta, scale produce proportionally.
  • Patrol of 8: 1.5 lb pasta — boil in two batches if the pot is too small.

Advancement Tie-In

  • Tenderfoot: assist in preparing this meal.
  • First Class: works as the lunch slot in a planned menu — note this is served cold, so it doesn't satisfy the Second Class "hot lunch" requirement even though the pasta itself is cooked.
  • Cooking merit badge: satisfies the camp stove cooking method requirement (the pasta is boiled, even though served cold).
