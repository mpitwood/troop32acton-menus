Campfire Quesadillas

Lunch · Main · Meat & Vegetarian (one station) · Nut-Free: Yes · Egg-Free: Yes · 2-Burner Stove · Serves 6 · Prep 10 min · Cook 15-20 min

Quick, hot, and easy to run meat and vegetarian versions side by side in two pans on the same 2-burner stove. Good midday lunch when the patrol wants something hot but not a long cook.

Ingredients (serves 6, ~1.5 quesadillas each)

  • 9 large flour tortillas
  • 2 cups shredded cheese (cheddar or Mexican blend)
  • 2 cups cooked shredded chicken (rotisserie or canned, drained)
  • 1 (15 oz) can black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 cup corn — canned (drained) is easiest since it needs no cooler space at all; fresh-cut or frozen-then-thawed both work too
  • 1 bell pepper, diced
  • Oil, for the pan
  • Salsa and sour cream, for serving

Shopping List

  • Flour tortillas, large, 9
  • Shredded cheese, 2 cups
  • Cooked shredded chicken, 2 cups (canned or rotisserie)
  • Black beans, 1 can (15 oz)
  • Corn, 1 cup (canned recommended)
  • Bell pepper, 1
  • Oil
  • Salsa, sour cream

Equipment

  • 2-burner stove
  • 1-2 skillets
  • Spatula, cutting board and knife

Instructions

Advance Prep (at home)

  • Decant the cooking oil into a small travel bottle — a few tablespoons is all this needs.
  • If using canned chicken and canned corn, no cooler space is needed for either — pack them as shelf-stable cans instead of pre-cooking and cooler-packing rotisserie chicken.

Onsite (at camp)

  1. Mix black beans and corn together for the vegetarian filling; keep chicken separate for the meat filling.
  2. Lightly oil a skillet over medium heat. Place a tortilla in the pan, sprinkle cheese on half, add chicken (or bean-corn mix) and a few diced peppers, fold the other half over.
  3. Cook 2-3 minutes per side until golden and cheese is melted. Repeat for remaining tortillas — run a second skillet on the other burner to do meat and vegetarian batches at the same time.
  4. Cut into wedges. Serve with salsa and sour cream.

Real Food Check

  • Protein: chicken, black beans, cheese
  • Vegetables: bell pepper, corn
  • Processed: tortillas are the one refined-grain item; no need to add more bread or chips on the side

Allergy & Diet Notes

  • Nut-free: yes, as written.
  • Egg-free: yes.

Cost Estimate

~$4.20/scout for 6: tortillas ~$3.50, cheese ~$3.75, chicken ~$7, beans ~$1.50, corn ~$1.50, pepper ~$1.75, salsa/sour cream ~$5.75. Boston-area pricing — see README.md for sourcing.

Scaling Notes

  • Patrol of 4: 6 tortillas, split filling proportionally.
  • Patrol of 8: 12 tortillas — run both skillets continuously, this is the meal most helped by having two burners going at once.

Advancement Tie-In

  • Tenderfoot: assist in preparing this meal.
  • Second Class: counts as the hot lunch a scout cooks solo.
  • First Class: works as the lunch slot in a planned menu.
  • Cooking merit badge: satisfies the camp stove cooking method requirement.
