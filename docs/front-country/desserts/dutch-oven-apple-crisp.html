Dutch Oven Apple Crisp

Dinner (dessert) · Dessert · Vegetarian · Nut-Free: Yes · Egg-Free: Yes · Dutch Oven · Serves 6 · Prep 15 min · Cook 35-40 min

Sliced apples under a buttery oat crumble — a change of pace from dutch-oven-peach-cobbler using the same dutch oven skills.

Ingredients (serves 6)

  • 6 apples, cored and sliced (peel on is fine, saves time)
  • 1/4 cup sugar
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • Topping: 1 cup rolled oats, 1/2 cup flour, 1/2 cup brown sugar, 1/2 cup (1 stick) butter, melted, 1/2 tsp cinnamon, pinch salt

Shopping List

  • Apples, 6
  • Sugar
  • Cinnamon
  • Lemon juice
  • Rolled oats, 1 cup
  • Flour, 1/2 cup
  • Brown sugar, 1/2 cup
  • Butter, 1 stick

Equipment

  • 12" dutch oven + lid
  • Charcoal chimney, ~24-26 coals
  • Lid lifter, leather gloves
  • Mixing bowl (for the topping)

Instructions

Advance Prep (at home)

  • Pre-mix the dry topping (oats, flour, brown sugar, cinnamon, salt) in a zip-top bag — at camp you just add melted butter and stir.
  • Decant the sugar, cinnamon, and lemon juice needed for the apples into small containers.
  • Core and slice the apples the morning of, tossed with a little extra lemon juice or water, to slow browning if not cooking right away.

Onsite (at camp)

  1. Light ~24-26 coals.
  2. Toss the apple slices with sugar, cinnamon, and lemon juice directly in the dutch oven.
  3. Stir the melted butter into the pre-mixed dry topping until crumbly. Sprinkle evenly over the apples.
  4. Cover. Set on 8 coals underneath, 16-17 coals on the lid. Bake 35-40 minutes, rotating the oven and lid a quarter turn every 10 minutes, until the apples are bubbly and the topping is golden.

Real Food Check

  • Protein: minimal
  • Fruit: whole apples
  • Processed: none — one of the more whole-food desserts in the repo; added sugar is present but there's no processed mix standing in for real ingredients

Allergy & Diet Notes

  • Nut-free: yes — no nuts in this topping (traditional apple crisp recipes sometimes add pecans or walnuts; this one doesn't).
  • Egg-free: yes.

Cost Estimate

~$2.30/scout for 6: apples ~$6.80, oats/flour/sugar/cinnamon/lemon juice ~$4.35, butter ~$2.75. Boston-area pricing — see README.md for sourcing.

Scaling Notes

  • Patrol of 4: 4 apples, 2/3 the topping.
  • Patrol of 8: 8 apples, 1.3x the topping — use a 14" oven if available, or expect a deeper fruit layer in a 12" oven with a slightly longer bake.

Advancement Tie-In

  • Tenderfoot: assist in preparing this meal.
  • Cooking merit badge: satisfies the dutch oven method and the dessert requirement for camp cooking in one dish.
