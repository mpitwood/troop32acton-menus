Dutch Oven Peach Cobbler

Dinner (dessert) · Dessert · Vegetarian · Nut-Free: Yes · Egg-Free: Yes · Dutch Oven · Serves 6 · Prep 5 min · Cook 35-40 min

The classic "dump cobbler" — canned peaches, dry cake mix, butter, no mixing bowl and no eggs. About as low-effort as a real dessert gets.

Ingredients (serves 6)

  • 2 (29 oz) cans sliced peaches, with juice
  • 1 box yellow cake mix (dry, straight from the box)
  • 1/2 cup (1 stick) butter, sliced thin
  • 1 tsp cinnamon

Shopping List

  • Canned sliced peaches, 2 cans (29 oz)
  • Yellow cake mix, 1 box
  • Butter, 1 stick
  • Cinnamon (check troop spice kit)

Equipment

  • 12" dutch oven + lid
  • Charcoal chimney, ~24-26 coals
  • Lid lifter, leather gloves

Instructions

Advance Prep (at home)

  • Decant the cinnamon into a small container or twist of foil — a teaspoon doesn't need the full jar along for the trip.
  • Slice the butter into pats at home so it's ready to dot straight onto the cobbler.

Onsite (at camp)

  1. Light ~24-26 coals.
  2. Pour both cans of peaches, with their juice, into the dutch oven.
  3. Sprinkle the dry cake mix evenly over the top — do not stir it in.
  4. Dot the butter slices evenly across the top, sprinkle with cinnamon.
  5. Cover. Set on 8 coals underneath, 16-17 coals on the lid. Bake 35-40 minutes, rotating the oven and lid a quarter turn every 10 minutes, until the top is golden and the peaches are bubbling underneath.

Real Food Check

  • Protein: minimal
  • Fruit: canned peaches (real fruit)
  • Processed: boxed cake mix, meaningful added sugar — an occasional treat, not a "real food" target dish

Allergy & Diet Notes

  • Nut-free: yes, as written.
  • Egg-free: yes — the dump-cobbler method uses no eggs at all.

Cost Estimate

~$2.05/scout for 6: peaches ~$6, cake mix ~$3.40, butter ~$2.75. Boston-area pricing — see README.md for sourcing.

Scaling Notes

  • Patrol of 4: 1.5 cans peaches, still use a full box of cake mix (a thin layer works fine, or save the extra dry mix for another cobbler).
  • Patrol of 8: 3 cans peaches, 1.5 boxes cake mix — use a 14" oven if available, or make two 12" ovens.

Advancement Tie-In

  • Tenderfoot: assist in preparing this meal.
  • Cooking merit badge: satisfies the dutch oven method and the dessert requirement for camp cooking in one dish.
