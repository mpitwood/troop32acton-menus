Campfire Banana Boats

Dinner (dessert) · Dessert · Vegetarian · Nut-Free: Yes (use a nut-free chocolate chip brand) · Egg-Free: Yes · Foil Pack/Coals · Serves 6 · Prep 5 min · Cook 5-8 min

Bananas stuffed with chocolate and marshmallow, warmed in the coals until gooey, eaten straight out of the peel with a spoon. Cheapest, fastest dessert in the repo.

Ingredients (serves 6)

  • 6 bananas
  • 1 cup nut-free mini chocolate chips
  • 1 cup mini marshmallows
  • Optional: crushed graham crackers, for topping

Shopping List

  • Bananas, 6
  • Mini chocolate chips (nut-free brand), 1 cup
  • Mini marshmallows, 1 cup
  • Optional: graham crackers
  • Heavy-duty foil

Equipment

  • Fire or coal bed
  • Heavy-duty aluminum foil
  • Spoons (each banana is eaten straight from the peel)

Instructions

Advance Prep (at home)

  • Portion the chocolate chips and marshmallows into small bags — no need to bring the full bags of either for 6 bananas' worth.
  • Bananas travel fine at room temperature or in the cooler; no special prep needed.

Onsite (at camp)

  1. Without peeling, slice each banana lengthwise through the peel, cutting about 3/4 of the way through (don't cut all the way).
  2. Gently open the slit and stuff with chocolate chips and mini marshmallows.
  3. Wrap each banana individually in foil.
  4. Set near the edge of the coals (not directly in the hottest part) for 5-8 minutes, until the chocolate and marshmallow are melted.
  5. Unwrap carefully — it's hot — top with crushed graham crackers if using, and eat with a spoon.

Real Food Check

  • Protein: minimal
  • Fruit: whole banana carries the dish
  • Processed: chocolate chips, marshmallows — a sugar-forward treat, but built on real produce rather than a boxed mix

Allergy & Diet Notes

  • Nut-free: the recipe itself is nut-free, but chocolate chips are commonly produced on shared lines with nuts — buy a brand that's specifically labeled nut-free/peanut-free if a scout with a nut allergy will be eating from the same bag.
  • Egg-free: yes.

Cost Estimate

~$1.15/scout for 6: bananas ~$2, chocolate chips ~$2.75, marshmallows ~$2. Boston-area pricing — see README.md for sourcing.

Scaling Notes

  • Patrol of 4: 4 bananas, scale chips/marshmallows proportionally.
  • Patrol of 8: 8 bananas — this scales linearly with no batching concerns, each banana is its own packet.

Advancement Tie-In

  • Tenderfoot: assist in preparing this meal.
  • Cooking merit badge: satisfies the foil-pack method and the dessert requirement for camp cooking in one dish.
