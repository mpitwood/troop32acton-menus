Hot Oatmeal Bar (Trail)

Breakfast · Main · Vegetarian/Vegan · Nut-Free: Yes · Egg-Free: Yes · Boil-and-Steep · Serves 1 · Weight: 5.3 oz/serving · ~613-683 kcal/serving (116-129 kcal/oz) · ~24-27g protein

One boil, done — the lowest-fuel hot breakfast on the menu. Everything is pre-mixed into a single per-person packet at home, so there's no measuring or combining at camp, just water. Sized with the protein side included to land close to Philmont's ~700-1,000 kcal breakfast range.

Ingredients (per person)

Base:

  • 2 oz instant oats
  • 0.3 oz brown sugar
  • Pinch cinnamon
  • 0.5 oz dried fruit (raisins, cranberries, or chopped apple)
  • 0.5 oz nonfat dry milk powder
  • 1 oz sunflower seeds

Protein side (choose one, not mixed in):

  • Meat: 1 jerky stick or meat stick (~1 oz)
  • Vegetarian: 1 sunflower seed butter packet (~1 oz)

Shopping List (for a patrol of 6)

  • Instant oats, ~12 oz
  • Brown sugar, cinnamon
  • Dried fruit, ~3 oz
  • Nonfat dry milk powder, ~3 oz
  • Sunflower seeds, ~6 oz
  • Jerky/meat sticks and sunflower seed butter packets — buy based on how many scouts want each, not one of each per scout

Equipment

  • Pot, stove, lighter/matches
  • Bowl or eat straight from the pot/bag

Instructions

Advance Prep (at home)

  • Combine the oats, sugar, cinnamon, dried fruit, milk powder, and sunflower seeds into one bag per person per breakfast day. This is the only assembly step — everything else happens at camp with hot water.

Onsite (at trail)

  1. Boil about 1 cup water per person.
  2. Pour into the oat packet (or a bowl), stir, cover, let sit 3–5 minutes until thickened.
  3. Add the protein side — a jerky stick for meat-eating scouts, a sunflower seed butter packet for vegetarian scouts — as a separate item, not stirred into the shared pot if cooking for the group at once.

Real Food Check

  • Protein: milk powder, sunflower seeds; + jerky or sunflower seed butter (protein side)
  • Whole food: oats, dried fruit
  • Processed: none significant in the base — brown sugar is the only added sugar, in a controlled small amount

Allergy & Diet Notes

  • Nut-free: yes — sunflower seeds and sunflower seed butter throughout, never peanut.
  • Egg-free: yes.
  • Vegan: yes, if using a vegan protein side and a plant milk powder.

Packaging & Trash Notes

  • Pre-mixed single-serving bags mean zero measuring trash at camp — just the one bag per person, reused as the eating vessel, then packed out.
  • Buy sunflower seeds and dried fruit in bulk and portion at home rather than buying pre-portioned pouches.

Cost Estimate

Base: ~$0.75/scout (oats ~$0.30, sugar/cinnamon ~$0.10, dried fruit ~$0.35, milk powder ~$0.15, sunflower seeds ~$0.45). Protein side: jerky/ meat stick ~$1.25 or sunflower seed butter packet ~$0.60. Boston-area pricing — see README.md for sourcing.

Scaling Notes

  • Patrol of 4/8: scales linearly, one packet per person per morning.
  • Keeps for the full 3-night trip with no special storage.

Advancement Tie-In

  • Tenderfoot: assist in preparing this meal.
  • Second Class: counts as a hot meal a scout cooks solo.
  • Cooking merit badge (trail cooking): a low-fuel trail-cooked breakfast.
