Sunflower Butter Noodles — Vegetarian Base + Chicken Boost

Dinner · Main · Vegetarian base · Nut-Free: Yes · Egg-Free: Yes · Boil-and-Steep · Serves 1 (base) + boost scaled to meat-eaters · Weight: 6 oz/serving base + ~1 oz/meat-eater · ~685 kcal/serving base (114 kcal/oz) + ~130 kcal boost · ~24g protein base + ~20g boost

A peanut-free take on peanut noodles — sunflower seed butter does the job peanut sauce usually does, with quick-cooking rice noodles that rehydrate with hot water instead of a real boil. TVP is stirred into the base so the vegetarian build isn't leaning on sunflower seed butter alone for protein.

Ingredients (per person)

Base:

  • 3.5 oz quick-cooking rice noodles or ramen noodles (discard the seasoning packet if using ramen — season separately)
  • 1.5 oz sunflower seed butter (in a packet or squeeze tube)
  • 0.5 oz TVP (textured vegetable protein)
  • 0.15 oz soy sauce powder (or a small liquid soy sauce packet)
  • Pinch rice vinegar powder, pinch sugar
  • 0.4 oz dehydrated carrot/snap pea flakes

Chicken boost (sized to meat-eating scouts):

  • 1 oz dehydrated or jerky-style chicken bits

Shopping List (for a patrol of 6)

  • Quick-cooking rice noodles or ramen, ~21 oz
  • Sunflower seed butter packets, ~9 oz
  • TVP, ~3 oz
  • Soy sauce powder or packets
  • Rice vinegar powder, sugar
  • Dehydrated carrot/snap pea flakes, ~2.5 oz
  • Dehydrated or jerky-style chicken bits — buy/prep based on headcount of meat-eaters

Equipment

  • Pot, stove, lighter/matches

Instructions

Advance Prep (at home)

  • Portion the noodles, TVP, soy sauce powder, vinegar powder, sugar, and veggie flakes into one bag per person per dinner day. Pack the sunflower seed butter packet alongside — it mixes in better if kept separate until serving.

Onsite (at trail)

  1. Boil water and pour over the noodle bag (or into a pot with the noodles and TVP), enough to cover. Cover and let sit 5–7 minutes until the noodles and TVP soften — check the specific noodle package for time.
  2. Drain excess water if needed. Stir in the sunflower seed butter, soy sauce powder, vinegar, sugar, and rehydrated veggie flakes until the sauce coats the noodles.
  3. Serve the vegetarian base first, straight from the shared pot. Meat-eating scouts then stir chicken bits into their own bowl.

Real Food Check

  • Protein (base): sunflower seed butter, TVP; + chicken (boost)
  • Vegetables: dehydrated carrot and snap pea flakes
  • Processed: rice noodles are a refined-grain base — the sunflower seed butter, TVP, and vegetables are doing the real nutritional work here

Allergy & Diet Notes

  • Nut-free: yes — sunflower seed butter, not peanut, throughout.
  • Egg-free: yes — check the specific noodle brand's label if using ramen-style noodles, most are egg-free.

Packaging & Trash Notes

  • Discard the ramen seasoning packet at home if using ramen noodles — no need to carry it out if it's never opened, and it's replaced by the soy sauce/vinegar/sugar mix anyway, which is lower sodium.
  • Buy sunflower seed butter in a larger jar and portion into small squeeze tubes or bags at home rather than carrying individual store-bought packets — cheaper and less packaging per ounce.

Cost Estimate

Base: ~$1.85/scout (noodles ~$0.70, sunflower seed butter ~$0.68, TVP ~$0.18, soy sauce powder ~$0.09, veggie flakes ~$0.40). Boost: ~$1.60 per meat-eating scout. Boston-area pricing — see README.md for sourcing.

Scaling Notes

  • Patrol of 4/8: scales linearly; boost sized to actual meat-eater headcount.
  • All base ingredients keep for the full trip length.

Advancement Tie-In

  • Tenderfoot: assist in preparing this meal.
  • Second Class: counts as a hot meal a scout cooks solo.
  • Cooking merit badge (trail cooking): a genuine trail-cooked dinner.
