Backcountry Quesadilla Night — Vegetarian Base + Pepperoni Boost

*Dinner · Main · Vegetarian base · Nut-Free: Yes · Egg-Free: Check tortilla label · Boil-and-Steep (for the base filling) · Serves 1 (base)

  • boost scaled to meat-eaters · Weight: 6 oz/serving base + ~1 oz/meat-eater · ~639 kcal/serving base (105 kcal/oz) + ~140 kcal boost · ~32g protein base + ~6g boost*

A change of pace from noodle-and-rice dinners — reconstituted cheese powder becomes a thick sauce/spread, folded into a tortilla with black beans and TVP, then warmed in a pan if you're carrying one, or eaten as a cold wrap if you're not.

Ingredients (per person)

Base:

  • 2 tortillas (3.2 oz)
  • 1 oz cheese powder
  • 1 oz dehydrated black bean flakes
  • 0.5 oz TVP (textured vegetable protein)
  • 0.4 oz dehydrated bell pepper/onion flakes

Pepperoni boost (sized to meat-eating scouts):

  • 1 oz sliced pepperoni

Shopping List (for a patrol of 6)

  • Tortillas, 12
  • Cheese powder, ~6 oz
  • Dehydrated black bean flakes, ~6 oz
  • TVP, ~3 oz
  • Dehydrated bell pepper/onion flakes, ~2.5 oz
  • Pepperoni — buy based on headcount of meat-eaters

Equipment

  • Pot, stove, lighter/matches
  • A small pan or the pot lid, if warming the quesadillas (optional — this works cold too)

Instructions

Advance Prep (at home)

  • Portion the cheese powder, bean flakes, TVP, and veggie flakes into one bag per person per dinner day.

Onsite (at trail)

  1. Boil a small amount of water — about 1/3 cup per person — and pour into the cheese powder/bean flake/TVP/veggie flake bag. Stir until it forms a thick, spreadable filling.
  2. Spread the filling on one tortilla, top with a second tortilla to make a quesadilla shape.
  3. Build the vegetarian quesadillas first. For meat-eating scouts, add pepperoni to the filling before closing the second tortilla.
  4. If you have a pan, warm each quesadilla 1–2 minutes per side over low heat until the tortilla crisps slightly. If not, it's still a perfectly good folded wrap eaten as-is.

Real Food Check

  • Protein (base): black beans, TVP, cheese powder; + pepperoni (boost)
  • Vegetables: dehydrated bell pepper and onion
  • Processed: cheese powder, TVP, and pepperoni are processed; tortillas are a refined grain — beans and vegetables are still doing real nutritional work in the base

Allergy & Diet Notes

  • Nut-free: yes.
  • Egg-free: most tortillas are egg-free — check the specific brand's label.

Packaging & Trash Notes

  • Buy tortillas in bulk packs and repackage into flat zip-top bags rather than carrying the original plastic sleeves — cuts bulk and reduces crushing in the pack.
  • Cheese powder, bean flakes, and TVP pre-combine into one bag per person, so this meal generates minimal camp trash beyond that bag.

Cost Estimate

Base: ~$2.13/scout (tortillas ~$0.70, cheese powder ~$0.50, black bean flakes ~$0.60, TVP ~$0.18, veggie flakes ~$0.40, rounding adjustments included). Boost: ~$1.15 per meat-eating scout. Boston-area pricing — see README.md for sourcing.

Scaling Notes

  • Patrol of 4/8: scales linearly; boost sized to actual meat-eater headcount.
  • Works with or without a pan, which makes it a good backup dinner if the patrol's cookware is minimal that trip.

Advancement Tie-In

  • Tenderfoot: assist in preparing this meal.
  • Second Class: counts as a hot meal a scout cooks solo.
  • Cooking merit badge (trail cooking): a genuine trail-cooked dinner.
