# Second Class Menu Planning Worksheet — Requirement 2e

**This is your worksheet, not a recipe page.**

- Requirement 2e asks *you* to plan, cook, and explain one hot meal — not
  read it off a page.
- Don't copy from a page's "Real Food Check" — read the **Ingredients**
  list yourself and work it out.
- Bring the finished worksheet to whoever signs off (usually a senior
  scout or your Scoutmaster — Second Class doesn't use merit badge
  counselors).

> **Requirement 2e, as written:** "On one campout, plan and cook one hot
> breakfast or lunch, selecting foods from MyPlate or the current USDA
> nutritional model. Explain the importance of good nutrition.
> Demonstrate how to transport, store, and prepare the foods you
> selected."

- This repo uses RealFood.gov, which replaced MyPlate in January 2026 —
  same idea. Ask your leader which term they want you to use.
- This is **one** meal — breakfast or lunch, not both, not dinner.

## 5. Explain the importance of good nutrition

- This asks you to explain good nutrition generally — not just justify
  this one meal.
- Prompts (you don't need all of them — just write a real paragraph):
  - Why does a growing teenager need protein, and where did you get it
    in this meal?
  - Why do vegetables and fruit matter, even on a short weekend campout?
  - What happens to a scout's energy on a hike or busy camp day without
    a real breakfast?
