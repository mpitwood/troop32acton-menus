# Second Class Menu Planning Worksheet — Requirement 2e

This is **your** worksheet, not a page from the recipe book. Requirement
2e asks you to plan, cook, and explain one hot meal — the recipe pages
give you ingredients and instructions, but the food-group sorting and
nutrition explanation below has to be your own work. Don't copy answers
from a page's "Real Food Check" section — read the **Ingredients** list
yourself and fill this in from scratch. Bring the finished worksheet to
whoever verifies this requirement for your troop (often a senior scout or
your Scoutmaster, not a merit badge counselor — Second Class doesn't use
counselors).

> **Requirement 2e, as written:** "On one campout, plan and cook one hot
> breakfast or lunch, selecting foods from MyPlate or the current USDA
> nutritional model. Explain the importance of good nutrition.
> Demonstrate how to transport, store, and prepare the foods you
> selected."

*(A note on wording: this troop's recipe book uses RealFood.gov and the
2025-2030 Dietary Guidelines, which replaced MyPlate in January 2026. The
leader signing off may still say "MyPlate" — same idea, ask which term
they want you to use.)*

This is **one** meal — breakfast or lunch, not both, and not dinner.

## 5. Explain the importance of good nutrition

This part of the requirement isn't asking you to justify this one meal
specifically — it's asking you to explain, in your own words, why good
nutrition matters generally. A few prompts to get you started (you don't
need to answer all of them, just write a real paragraph):

- Why does a growing teenager need protein, and where did you get it in
  this meal?
- Why do vegetables and fruit matter, even on a short weekend campout?
- What happens to a scout's energy on a hike or busy camp day if they
  skip a real breakfast?
