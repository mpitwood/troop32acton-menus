# Protein Bar & Jerky Station

*Any meal · Snack · Meat & Vegetarian (one station) · Nut-Free: Check bar
label · Egg-Free: Check bar label · No-Cook · Serves 1 (per day) ·
Weight: ~3 oz/serving · ~350-380 kcal/serving (~117-127 kcal/oz)*

A second daily snack alongside the trail mix — a bar plus a protein side,
same "pick your own" pattern as the lunch bars, so nobody's protein
option is an afterthought.

## Ingredients (per person, per day)

- 1 protein or energy bar (~2 oz)

**Protein side (choose one):**
- Meat: 1 jerky stick (~1 oz)
- Vegetarian: 1 plant-based jerky stick (~1 oz) or 1 sunflower seed
  butter packet (~1 oz, cheaper option)

## Shopping List (for a patrol of 6, per day)

- [ ] Protein/energy bars, 6 (nut-free brand recommended)
- [ ] Jerky sticks, plant-based jerky, and/or sunflower seed butter
      packets — buy based on how many scouts want each

## Equipment

- None

## Instructions

### Advance Prep (at home)

- Buy bars in a bulk box and strip the outer cardboard box before the
  trip — the individually wrapped bars themselves are worth keeping
  wrapped (protects against crushing), just don't carry the box.

### Onsite (at trail)

1. Eat throughout the day or at breaks — no prep needed.

## Real Food Check

- **Protein:** varies by bar brand and protein side chosen
- **Processed:** bars and jerky are both processed convenience foods —
  standard and reasonable for a backpacking snack, not something to
  scrutinize the way a front-country meal would be

## Allergy & Diet Notes

- Nut-free: check the specific bar brand — many contain almonds or
  peanuts; buy a nut-free-labeled bar as the troop default.
- Egg-free: check the specific bar brand.
- Vegetarian: plant-based jerky is the closer texture match to meat
  jerky; sunflower seed butter packets are the cheaper option if budget
  is tight.

## Packaging & Trash Notes

- Strip the outer box at home — see Advance Prep. This is the main
  packaging-reduction move for this snack; individual bar/jerky wrappers
  are worth keeping as-is.
- Plant-based jerky and meat jerky packaging are both foil-lined and
  won't burn — pack out, don't burn.

## Cost Estimate

Meat build: ~$2.75/scout/day (bar ~$1.50, jerky ~$1.25). Vegetarian
build: ~$3.25/scout/day with plant-based jerky (~$1.75) or ~$2.10/scout/
day with sunflower seed butter (~$0.60) — plant-based jerky runs pricier
per ounce than meat jerky, worth knowing before defaulting to it for
budget-tight trips. Boston-area pricing — see
[README.md](../README.md#pricing) for sourcing.

## Scaling Notes

- Patrol of 4/8: scales linearly per person per day.
- Multiply by trip length; bars and jerky both keep for the full trip
  with no special storage.
