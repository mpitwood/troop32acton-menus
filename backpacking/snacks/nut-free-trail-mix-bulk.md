# Nut-Free Trail Mix (Bulk Repackaged)

*Any meal · Snack · Vegan · Nut-Free: Yes · Egg-Free: Yes · No-Cook ·
Serves 1 (per day) · Weight: ~3.5 oz/serving · ~440 kcal/serving (~125
kcal/oz)*

The default all-day snack — high calorie density for the weight, and
built nut-free from the ingredient list itself rather than by
substitution. Repackaged from bulk bins at home, not individually
wrapped store bags.

## Ingredients (per person, per day)

- 1 oz sunflower seeds, roasted and salted
- 1 oz dried fruit (raisins, cranberries, or a mix)
- 0.75 oz pretzel pieces
- 0.75 oz nut-free chocolate chips

## Shopping List (for a patrol of 6, per day)

- [ ] Sunflower seeds, bulk, ~6 oz
- [ ] Dried fruit, bulk, ~6 oz
- [ ] Pretzel pieces, bulk, ~4.5 oz
- [ ] Nut-free chocolate chips, bulk, ~4.5 oz

## Equipment

- None

## Instructions

### Advance Prep (at home)

- Buy all four ingredients from bulk bins, not pre-portioned bags. Mix
  the whole batch for the trip in a large bowl, then portion into one bag
  per person per day — or into a shared bag per cook group if the patrol
  trusts everyone to take a fair share.

### Onsite (at trail)

1. Eat throughout the day from the portioned bag — no prep needed.

## Real Food Check

- **Protein:** sunflower seeds
- **Fruit:** dried fruit
- **Processed:** pretzels and chocolate chips are processed, in a
  controlled ratio against the whole-food seeds and fruit — this mix
  leans less candy-heavy than a typical store trail mix

## Allergy & Diet Notes

- Nut-free: built to be nut-free from the ingredient list, not by
  substitution — still check the chocolate chip brand for shared-facility
  warnings.
- Egg-free: yes.
- Vegan: yes.

## Packaging & Trash Notes

- This is the single biggest packaging-reduction opportunity in the
  repo: buying from bulk bins and mixing one large batch at home replaces
  what would otherwise be four separate store bags per ingredient, per
  person, per day.
- A shared per-cook-group bag (rather than strictly personal bags)
  further cuts bag count — worth it if the group is good about sharing
  fairly; switch to personal bags if that becomes an issue.
