# Cold-Soak Muesli

*Breakfast · Main · Vegetarian (vegan option) · Nut-Free: Yes · Egg-Free:
Yes · Cold-Soak · Serves 1 · Weight: ~6.4 oz/serving · ~704-774
kcal/serving (~110-121 kcal/oz) · ~28-31g protein*

Zero fuel, ready when you wake up. Mix it in a bag the night before, add
cold water, and by morning it's soft and ready to eat straight from the
bag — good for alpine starts when lighting a stove costs you daylight.
Sized to land near Philmont's own breakfast calorie and protein numbers
(see [README.md](../README.md)), not a lighter "diet" version of one.

## Ingredients (per person)

**Base:**
- 3/4 cup rolled oats (2.25 oz)
- 3 tbsp nonfat dry milk powder (0.75 oz)
- 1/4 cup dried cranberries or raisins (1 oz)
- 2 tbsp chia seeds or ground flax (0.6 oz)
- 2 tbsp sunflower seeds (0.5 oz)
- 1 tbsp brown sugar (0.3 oz)
- Pinch cinnamon, pinch salt

**Protein side (choose one):**
- Meat: 1 jerky stick (~1 oz)
- Vegetarian: 1 sunflower seed butter packet (~1 oz)

## Shopping List (for a patrol of 6)

- [ ] Rolled oats, ~4.5 cups
- [ ] Nonfat dry milk powder, ~1 cup
- [ ] Dried cranberries or raisins, ~1.5 cups
- [ ] Chia seeds or ground flax, ~12 tbsp
- [ ] Sunflower seeds, ~12 tbsp
- [ ] Brown sugar, cinnamon, salt (small amounts)
- [ ] Jerky sticks and sunflower seed butter packets — buy based on how
      many scouts want each

## Equipment

- Wide-mouth bottle or a sturdy zip-top bag per person

## Instructions

### Advance Prep (at home)

- Portion each person's oats, milk powder, dried fruit, chia, sunflower
  seeds, sugar, cinnamon, and salt into its own quart zip-top bag. Label
  with the day. This is the entire "cooking" — do it once at home for the
  whole trip.

### Onsite (at trail)

1. The night before (or first thing in the morning), add 3/4 to 1 cup
   cold water to the bag, seal, and shake to mix.
2. Let sit at least 15–30 minutes (overnight is fine and softens it more).
   Eat straight from the bag — no pot, no stove, no cleanup beyond the bag
   itself.
3. Eat the protein side alongside — jerky stick or sunflower seed butter
   packet.

## Real Food Check

- **Protein:** milk powder, chia seeds, sunflower seeds; + jerky or
  sunflower seed butter (protein side)
- **Whole food:** rolled oats, dried fruit
- **Processed:** none in the base — about as close to a whole-food
  breakfast as backpacking gets

## Allergy & Diet Notes

- Nut-free: yes.
- Egg-free: yes.
- Vegan: swap the dairy milk powder for a powdered plant milk, and use
  the sunflower seed butter protein side.

## Packaging & Trash Notes

- The zip-top bag it's mixed in doubles as the bowl — no separate dish to
  pack out. Reuse the same bag for 2–3 mornings before it needs replacing
  if it's rinsed and dried between uses.
- Buy oats, milk powder, dried fruit, chia, and sunflower seeds in bulk
  and portion at home rather than buying pre-portioned single-serve
  pouches — cuts both cost and the number of individual wrappers
  generated.

chia ~$0.25, sunflower seeds ~$0.20). Protein side: jerky ~$1.25 or

## Scaling Notes

- Patrol of 4/8: scales linearly, one bag per person per morning.
- Portioned bags keep for the full 3-night trip unrefrigerated — this is
  a good "make all of them at once before you leave" recipe.
