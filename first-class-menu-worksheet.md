# First Class Menu Planning Worksheet — Requirement 2a

This is **your** worksheet, not a page from the recipe book. Requirement
2a asks you to plan a menu and explain how it meets nutritional needs —
the recipe pages give you ingredients and instructions, but the food-group
sorting and nutrition reasoning below has to be your own work. Don't copy
answers from a page's "Real Food Check" section — read the **Ingredients**
list yourself and fill this in from scratch. Bring the finished worksheet
to whoever verifies this requirement for your troop (often a senior scout
or your Scoutmaster, not a merit badge counselor — First Class doesn't use
counselors).

> **Requirement 2a, as written:** "Help plan a menu for one of the above
> campouts that includes at least one breakfast, one lunch, and one
> dinner, and that requires cooking at least two of the meals. Tell how
> the menu includes the foods from MyPlate or the current USDA nutritional
> model and how it meets nutritional needs for the planned activity or
> campout."

*(A note on wording: this troop's recipe book uses RealFood.gov and the
2025-2030 Dietary Guidelines, which replaced MyPlate in January 2026. Your
Scoutmaster or the leader signing off may still say "MyPlate" — same
idea, ask which term they want you to use.)*

## 1. The campout

"One of the above campouts" means one of the outdoor overnight campouts
you're counting toward First Class requirement 1a — not a hypothetical
trip. Pick one you've actually been on or have booked.

- Campout: ________________________ Date(s): ________________________
- Trip type: ☐ Front-country (car camping) ☐ Backpacking
- Nights: _____
- Activity level: ☐ Easy / relaxed ☐ Moderate (day hikes, active program)
  ☐ Strenuous (long miles, elevation gain, cold weather)

*Activity level matters in Section 5 — a strenuous backpacking day needs
more food than a relaxed weekend in the campground.*

## What comes next

This same menu carries forward into the rest of the Cooking section of
First Class, so don't lose this worksheet:

- **2b** — build a budget and food-amount list from this menu, for
  feeding three or more youth, and secure the ingredients.
- **2c** — show which pans, utensils, and other gear this menu needs.
- **2d** — demonstrate safe handling/storage of perishables and proper
  disposal of camp garbage and waste water.
- **2e** — actually cook this menu on a campout: serve as cook, supervise
  your assistant(s), and supervise cleanup.
