# Dutch Oven Apple Crisp

*Dinner (dessert) · Dessert · Vegetarian · Nut-Free: Yes · Egg-Free: Yes ·
Dutch Oven · Serves 6 · Prep 15 min · Cook 35-40 min*

Sliced apples under a buttery oat crumble — a change of pace from
[dutch-oven-peach-cobbler](dutch-oven-peach-cobbler.md) using the same
dutch oven skills.

## Ingredients (serves 6)

- 6 apples, cored and sliced (peel on is fine, saves time)
- 1/4 cup sugar
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- **Topping:** 1 cup rolled oats, 1/2 cup flour, 1/2 cup brown sugar,
  1/2 cup (1 stick) butter, melted, 1/2 tsp cinnamon, pinch salt

## Equipment

- 12" dutch oven + lid
- Charcoal chimney, ~24-26 coals
- Lid lifter, leather gloves
- Mixing bowl (for the topping)

## Instructions

### Advance Prep (at home)

- Pre-mix the dry topping (oats, flour, brown sugar, cinnamon, salt) in a
  zip-top bag — at camp you just add melted butter and stir.
- Decant the sugar, cinnamon, and lemon juice needed for the apples into
  small containers.
- Core and slice the apples the morning of, tossed with a little extra
  lemon juice or water, to slow browning if not cooking right away.

### Onsite (at camp)

1. Light ~24-26 coals.
2. Toss the apple slices with sugar, cinnamon, and lemon juice directly in
   the dutch oven.
3. Stir the melted butter into the pre-mixed dry topping until crumbly.
   Sprinkle evenly over the apples.
4. Cover. Set on 8 coals underneath, 16-17 coals on the lid. Bake 35-40
   minutes, rotating the oven and lid a quarter turn every 10 minutes,
   until the apples are bubbly and the topping is golden.

## Allergy & Diet Notes

- **Nut-free:** yes — no nuts in this topping (traditional apple crisp
  recipes sometimes add pecans or walnuts; this one doesn't).
- **Egg-free:** yes.

## Scaling Notes

- **Patrol of 4:** 4 apples, 2/3 the topping.
- **Patrol of 8:** 8 apples, 1.3x the topping — use a 14" oven if
  available, or expect a deeper fruit layer in a 12" oven with a slightly
  longer bake.
