# Campfire Pie Iron Fruit Pies

*Dinner (dessert) · Dessert · Vegetarian · Nut-Free: Yes · Egg-Free: Check
bread/filling labels · Pie Iron/Coals · Serves 6 · Prep 10 min · Cook 5-8
min per pie (batches depend on how many pie irons the troop owns)*

Bread, butter, and pie filling pressed in a pie iron and held right in the
coals until golden and crisp — a hands-on dessert every scout cooks their
own version of.

## Ingredients (serves 6)

- 12 slices white sandwich bread
- Butter, softened
- 1 (21 oz) can fruit pie filling (cherry, apple, or blueberry)
- Powdered sugar, for dusting (optional)
- Cooking spray, for the irons

## Shopping List

- [ ] Sandwich bread, 1 loaf (12 slices)
- [ ] Butter
- [ ] Fruit pie filling, 1 can (21 oz)
- [ ] Optional: powdered sugar
- [ ] Cooking spray

## Equipment

- Pie irons — as many as the troop owns; fewer irons means more batches
- Fire or coal bed

## Instructions

### Advance Prep (at home)

- Butter one side of each bread slice ahead of time and stack with
  parchment or wax paper between slices — one less step at camp.
- Portion the powdered sugar into a small shaker or bag if dusting the
  pies at the end.

### Onsite (at camp)

1. Spray the pie iron. Place one bread slice, butter-side down, in the
   iron. Spoon 2-3 tbsp of pie filling in the center.
2. Top with a second slice, butter-side up. Close the iron and trim any
   excess bread hanging out the sides.
3. Cook directly in the coals (or held just above them) 3-4 minutes per
   side, until golden and crisp.
4. Carefully open the iron — filling is very hot. Repeat for remaining
   pies with however many irons the troop has.
5. Dust with powdered sugar before serving.

## Real Food Check

- **Protein:** minimal
- **Processed:** canned pie filling and white sandwich bread are both
  processed convenience items — this is a treat, not a "real food"
  standard-bearer. It earns its place for the hands-on cooking experience.

## Allergy & Diet Notes

- **Nut-free:** yes, as written.
- **Egg-free:** most sandwich bread and canned pie filling are egg-free —
  check the specific brands.

## Cost Estimate

~$1.70/scout for 6: bread ~$4.25, butter ~$0.75, pie filling ~$4.50,
powdered sugar ~$0.75. Boston-area pricing — see
[README.md](../../README.md#pricing-basis) for sourcing.

## Scaling Notes

- **Patrol of 4/8:** scales linearly (2 bread slices + filling per pie) —
  the real constraint is how many pie irons the troop owns, not
  ingredients. With only 2-3 irons, budget extra time for batches.

## Advancement Tie-In

- **Cooking merit badge:** a pie iron does **not** satisfy the foil-pack or
  skewer method requirement — it's neither. Use this as a fun dessert on
  its own, and pair the meal's main course with a true
  [foil-pack recipe](../dinner/foil-packet-fajitas.md) if a scout still
