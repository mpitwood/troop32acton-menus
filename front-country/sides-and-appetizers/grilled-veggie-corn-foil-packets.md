# Grilled Veggie & Corn Foil Packets

*Any meal · Side · Vegan · Nut-Free: Yes · Egg-Free: Yes · Foil Pack/Coals
· Serves 6 · Prep 10 min · Cook 15-20 min*

A colorful, genuinely tasty side that cooks on the same coals as whatever
foil-packet main the patrol is making — no extra fire, no extra pot, works
for every diet in the patrol without modification.

## Ingredients (serves 6)

- 4 ears corn, kernels cut off (or 2 cups canned corn, drained — easier to
  pack than fresh cobs and needs no cooler space)
- 2 zucchini, sliced into half-moons
- 1 bell pepper, sliced
- 1 red onion, sliced
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- Salt and pepper
- Optional: lime wedges, chopped cilantro, grated parmesan or cotija (skip
  for vegan)

## Instructions

### Advance Prep (at home)

- Slice the zucchini, bell pepper, and onion ahead — all hold up fine in a
  cooler for a day or two. Cut corn off the cob at home too, if using
  fresh, so it's ready to toss straight into the packets.
- Decant the olive oil and garlic powder into small travel containers.

### Onsite (at camp)

1. Toss all vegetables with olive oil, garlic powder, salt, and pepper.
2. Divide evenly across 2-3 double-layered foil packets. Seal tightly.
3. Place on a grate over medium-hot coals. Cook 15-20 minutes, flipping
   once, until vegetables are tender.
4. Open carefully. Finish with a squeeze of lime and cheese if using.
