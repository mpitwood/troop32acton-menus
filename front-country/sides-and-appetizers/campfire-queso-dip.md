# Campfire Queso Dip

*Any meal · Appetizer · Vegetarian base, meat add-in optional · Nut-Free:
Yes · Egg-Free: Yes · 2-Burner Stove · Serves 6 · Prep 5 min · Cook 10 min*

A five-minute melt that turns a bag of chips into an actual appetizer —
good for the wait between arriving at camp and dinner being ready.

## Ingredients (serves 6, as a shared appetizer)

- 1 (16 oz) block processed melting cheese (e.g. Velveeta-style), cubed
- 1 (10 oz) can diced tomatoes with green chilies (e.g. Rotel), undrained
- Optional: 1/2 lb cooked, crumbled chorizo or ground beef (keep separate
  so vegetarian scouts can dip before it's added)
- Tortilla chips, for serving

## Shopping List

- [ ] Processed melting cheese, 1 block (16 oz)
- [ ] Diced tomatoes with green chilies, 1 can (10 oz)
- [ ] Optional: chorizo or ground beef
- [ ] Tortilla chips, 1-2 bags

## Equipment

- 2-burner stove
- Medium pot
- Wooden spoon

## Instructions

### Advance Prep (at home)

- Cube the cheese block ahead so it's ready to go straight into the pot.
- If using chorizo or ground beef, brown it at home and cooler-pack
  separately — camp prep is then just reheating.

### Onsite (at camp)

1. In the pot over low-medium heat, combine the cubed cheese and undrained
   tomatoes.
2. Stir frequently until fully melted and smooth, about 8-10 minutes. Keep
   heat low — this scorches fast if left unattended.
3. If using meat, warm it separately and keep it in its own small bowl —
   never stir it into the pot of queso. Meat-eating scouts spoon it onto
   their own chips after dipping from the shared pot.
4. Serve immediately with chips — it thickens as it cools.

## Real Food Check

- **Protein:** cheese (+ chorizo/ground beef add-in, optional)
- **Vegetables:** tomatoes and chilies (via canned Rotel) only
- **Processed:** the most processed recipe in the repo — processed melting
  cheese is exactly what the current guidelines push away from. Swap in
  real shredded cheddar + a splash of milk for a less-processed version.

## Allergy & Diet Notes

- **Nut-free:** yes, as written.
- **Egg-free:** yes.
- **Vegetarian:** keep any meat add-in in its own bowl, never stirred into
  the shared pot — that keeps the dip itself safe for a vegetarian scout
  through the whole appetizer, including seconds.

## Scaling Notes

- **Patrol of 4:** 3/4 block cheese, 3/4 can tomatoes.
- **Patrol of 8:** 1.5-2 blocks cheese, 2 cans tomatoes — melt in two
  batches so the pot doesn't scorch trying to melt too much at once.
