# Dutch Oven Garlic Biscuits

*Any meal · Side · Vegetarian · Nut-Free: Yes · Egg-Free: Check label ·
Dutch Oven · Serves 6 · Prep 5 min · Cook 18-20 min*

The simplest possible dutch oven side — refrigerated biscuit dough, baked
in coals, brushed with garlic butter. Good alongside chili or pasta night
to round out the meal without adding real cook-time complexity.

## Ingredients (serves 6, ~2 biscuits each)

- 2 (16.3 oz) cans refrigerated biscuit dough
- 4 tbsp butter, melted
- 2 cloves garlic, minced (or 1 tsp garlic powder)
- 1 tbsp chopped parsley (optional)
- Oil, for the dutch oven

## Shopping List

- [ ] Refrigerated biscuit dough, 2 cans
- [ ] Butter
- [ ] Garlic (or garlic powder)
- [ ] Optional: parsley
- [ ] Oil

## Equipment

- 12" dutch oven + lid
- Charcoal chimney, ~24-26 coals
- Lid lifter, leather gloves
- Pastry brush (or a spoon works fine)

## Instructions

### Advance Prep (at home)

- Decant the garlic powder (if using instead of fresh) and dried parsley
  into a small container.
- The biscuit dough needs to stay refrigerated/cooler-cold until use; buy
  it close to departure.

### Onsite (at camp)

1. Light ~24-26 coals.
2. Lightly oil the dutch oven. Arrange the biscuits in a single layer,
   sides touching.
3. Cover. Set on 8 coals underneath, 16-18 coals on the lid. Bake 18-20
   minutes, rotating the oven and lid a quarter turn every 5-7 minutes,
   until golden and cooked through in the center.
4. Mix melted butter with garlic and parsley. Brush over the hot biscuits
   before serving.

## Real Food Check

- **Protein:** minimal
- **Processed:** refrigerated biscuit dough — a refined-flour convenience
  item
- Fine as an occasional side; don't pair with another refined-carb-heavy
  side in the same meal if trying to keep that meal protein/vegetable-
  forward

## Allergy & Diet Notes

- **Nut-free:** yes, as written.
- **Egg-free:** most refrigerated biscuit doughs (e.g. standard Pillsbury
  biscuits) are egg-free, but always check the specific product's
  ingredient label before buying.

## Scaling Notes

- **Patrol of 4:** 1 can of biscuits is enough.
- **Patrol of 8:** 3 cans — bake in two batches, or use a 14" oven if the
  troop has one, since a 12" oven only comfortably fits about 16 biscuits
  in one layer.
