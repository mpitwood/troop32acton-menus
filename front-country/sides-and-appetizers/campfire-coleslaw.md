# Campfire Coleslaw

*Any meal · Side · Vegan · Nut-Free: Yes · Egg-Free: Yes · No-Cook ·
Serves 6 · Prep 15 min · Cook: none*

A quick, crunchy, no-cook side that cuts the richness of a chili or pizza
night. A vinegar-based dressing instead of mayo keeps it egg-free and
travels better in a cooler.

## Ingredients (serves 6)

- 1 (14 oz) bag shredded coleslaw mix (cabbage and carrot)
- 1/4 cup apple cider vinegar
- 2 tbsp oil
- 2 tbsp sugar or honey
- 1 tsp celery seed (optional)
- Salt, pepper

## Shopping List

- [ ] Shredded coleslaw mix, 1 bag (14 oz)
- [ ] Apple cider vinegar
- [ ] Oil
- [ ] Sugar or honey
- [ ] Celery seed (optional)

## Equipment

- Large bowl or gallon zip-top bag
- Small jar or container (for the dressing)

## Instructions

### Advance Prep (at home)

- Whisk the vinegar, oil, sugar, celery seed, salt, and pepper together
  and pour into a small travel jar — no need to bring full bottles of
  vinegar and oil for 2 tbsp each.
- Toss the dressing with the coleslaw mix the night before if possible —
  it's better after 30+ minutes and travels fine cold in the cooler.

### Onsite (at camp)

1. If not already dressed at home, whisk the dressing ingredients together
   and toss with the coleslaw mix.
2. Let sit at least 30 minutes before serving if there's time, so the
   cabbage softens and absorbs the dressing.
