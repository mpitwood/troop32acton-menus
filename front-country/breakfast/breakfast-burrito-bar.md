# Breakfast Burrito Bar — Vegetarian Base + Sausage Boost

*Breakfast · Main · Vegetarian/Vegan base · Nut-Free: Yes · Egg-Free: Yes ·
2-Burner Stove · Serves 6 · Prep 15 min (less if sweet potatoes are
par-cooked at home) · Cook 15-20 min*

One spiced sweet potato and black bean filling, shared by the whole patrol
— it's egg-free and vegan-friendly by default, not a workaround. Sausage is
browned separately in a small pan and offered as a spoon-in topping only
for scouts who want it.

## Ingredients

**Base (serves 6, ~2 burritos each):**
- 2 large sweet potatoes, diced small (1/2" cubes)
- 2 (15 oz) cans black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 bell pepper, diced
- 1/2 onion, diced
- 1 cup shredded cheddar or pepper jack cheese (use a dairy-free shred for
  vegan)
- 12 burrito-size flour tortillas
- Oil, salt, pepper
- Optional: avocado, salsa, hot sauce

**Meat boost (sized to meat-eating scouts):**
- 3/4 lb breakfast sausage (covers up to 5-6 meat-eating scouts)

## Equipment

- 2-burner stove
- Large skillet with lid (base), small skillet (sausage)
- Spatula, and a second spatula dedicated to the sausage

## Instructions

### Advance Prep (at home)

- Dice the sweet potatoes and microwave or par-boil 4-5 minutes until just
  starting to soften, then cool and cooler-pack. Cuts camp cook time
  roughly in half.
- Decant the cumin, chili powder, and smoked paprika into one small spice
  container — no need to bring three full jars for a few teaspoons total.
- If bringing hot sauce or salsa just for topping, pour a small amount into
  a travel bottle instead of packing the full container.

### Onsite (at camp)

1. On one burner, heat oil in the large skillet. Add sweet potato, cover,
   cook 8-12 minutes (less if par-cooked), stirring occasionally, until
   fork-tender.
2. Add onion and pepper, cook 3-4 minutes. Stir in black beans, cumin,
   chili powder, smoked paprika, salt, pepper. Cook 4-5 minutes.
3. On the other burner, brown the sausage in the small skillet, breaking
   it up. Keep it in its own bowl — this is the topping, not an ingredient
   in the base.
4. Warm tortillas. **Assemble the vegetarian burrito(s) first**, straight
   from the base skillet: sweet potato-bean mixture, cheese, optional
   avocado/salsa. Then meat-eating scouts build their own burritos the same
   way, adding a scoop of sausage from its own bowl.

## Real Food Check

- **Protein (base):** black beans, cheese
- **Vegetables:** sweet potato, bell pepper, onion
- **Processed:** none in the base; sausage boost is a processed meat and
  optional — the base holds up as a complete dish without it

## Allergy & Diet Notes

- **Nut-free:** yes.
- **Egg-free:** yes — the base recipe contains no egg at all.
- **Vegan:** yes, with dairy-free cheese or no cheese.

~$1, bell pepper/onion ~$3.05, cheese ~$1.90, tortillas ~$4.40). Sausage
boost: ~$0.85 per meat-eating scout (3/4 lb sausage ~$4.70, split ~5-6

## Scaling Notes

- **Patrol of 4:** 1.5 sweet potatoes, 1.5 cans black beans, 8 tortillas;
  sausage sized to however many of the 4 eat meat.
- **Patrol of 8:** 3 sweet potatoes, 3 cans black beans, 16 tortillas —
  cook the sweet potato in two batches so the skillet isn't overcrowded;
  sausage sized per actual meat-eater headcount.
