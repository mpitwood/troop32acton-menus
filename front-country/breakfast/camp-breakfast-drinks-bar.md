# Camp Breakfast Drinks Bar

*Breakfast · Side · Vegetarian/Vegan option · Nut-Free: Check label · Egg-Free: Yes ·
2-Burner Stove · Serves 6 · Prep 5 min · Cook 5 min*

Cold mornings want a hot drink, warm mornings want a cold one — this bar
covers both instead of the patrol fighting over whatever one flavor
someone brought. Popular picks: hot chocolate, apple cider, and Arizona
iced tea (straight from the cooler, no prep). Pairs with any Main on this
page — this isn't a meal by itself.

## Ingredients (serves 6)

- 6 packets hot chocolate mix
- 6 packets apple cider mix (or a jug of apple cider, warmed on the stove)
- 6 cans or bottles Arizona iced tea, any flavor, chilled
- Water, for the hot chocolate and cider mixes

## Shopping List

- [ ] Hot chocolate mix, one 6-packet box
- [ ] Apple cider mix, one 6-packet box (or a jug of apple cider)
- [ ] Arizona iced tea, 6 cans or bottles

## Equipment

- 2-burner stove
- Pot for boiling water
- Mugs or cups, one per scout
- Cooler space for the Arizona cans

## Instructions

### Advance Prep (at home)

- Pre-portion hot chocolate and cider packets into one zip bag so nobody
  digs through two whole boxes at camp.
- Chill the Arizona cans overnight before the trip so they're cold without
  eating into cooler ice meant for food.

### Onsite (at camp)

1. Boil about 6 cups of water on the stove.
2. Let each scout pick hot chocolate or apple cider — stir one packet
   into 6-8 oz of hot water per mug.
3. Keep the Arizona cans in the cooler for scouts who'd rather have a
   cold drink.
4. Refill the pot and repeat for seconds.

## Real Food Check

- **Processed:** hot chocolate mix and cider mix are both instant with
  added sugar; Arizona iced tea is a sweetened bottled drink — this page
  is a treat/comfort item, not a nutrition anchor for the meal
- Pair with a protein-forward Main (eggs, sausage, breakfast burrito)
  rather than treating a hot chocolate as breakfast on its own

## Allergy & Diet Notes

- **Nut-free:** check the hot chocolate mix label specifically — many
  mass-market brands are processed on shared lines with tree nuts. Cider
  mix and Arizona are generally nut-free as written.
- **Egg-free:** yes, none of these contain egg.
- **Vegetarian/Vegan:** hot chocolate mix sometimes contains milk powder
  (vegetarian, not vegan) — buy a dairy-free cocoa mix if a vegan scout
  wants the hot option. Cider and Arizona are vegan as written.

## Cost Estimate

Hot chocolate mix $3.50/box (6 packets), apple cider mix $3.20/box (6
packets), Arizona iced tea $0.85/can x 6 = $5.10. All three options
stocked and split across 6 scouts: about $1.97/scout — cheaper if the
patrol only stocks two of the three.

## Scaling Notes

- **Patrol of 4:** same box sizes (already sold in multi-packet boxes) —
  just fewer packets left over, or save them for the next trip.
- **Patrol of 8:** buy two boxes of whichever mix is more popular, and 8
  cans of Arizona.

## Advancement Tie-In

- **Tenderfoot:** assist in preparing this as part of a breakfast.
- **Cooking merit badge:** boiling water on a camp stove counts toward
  the stove cooking method requirement if the Main it's paired with
  doesn't already use one.
