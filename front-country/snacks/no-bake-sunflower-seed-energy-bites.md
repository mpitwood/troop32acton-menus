# No-Bake Sunflower Seed Energy Bites

*Any meal · Snack (good pre-hike) · Vegan (with maple syrup) · Nut-Free:
Yes (check sunflower seed butter and chocolate chip brands) · Egg-Free:
Yes · No-Cook · Serves 6 (makes ~18-20 bites) · Prep 15 min · Cook: none*

Oats, sunflower seed butter, and honey rolled into bites — a genuinely
useful energy snack before a hike, not just a treat, and a good hands-on
patrol activity to make together.

## Ingredients (serves 6)

- 1.5 cups rolled oats
- 1/2 cup sunflower seed butter
- 1/3 cup honey or maple syrup
- 1/3 cup mini chocolate chips (nut-free brand)
- 1 tsp vanilla extract (optional)
- Pinch of salt

## Shopping List

- [ ] Rolled oats, 1.5 cups
- [ ] Sunflower seed butter, 1/2 cup
- [ ] Honey or maple syrup, 1/3 cup
- [ ] Mini chocolate chips (nut-free brand), 1/3 cup
- [ ] Vanilla extract (optional)

## Equipment

- Large mixing bowl
- Cooler, for chilling (optional)

## Instructions

### Advance Prep (at home)

- Decant the honey/maple syrup and vanilla extract into small travel
  containers — only a third of a cup and a teaspoon are needed.
- Can be fully mixed and rolled at home the night before, then
  cooler-transported — one less task at camp.

### Onsite (at camp)

1. Combine all ingredients in the bowl. Stir until well mixed and the
   mixture holds together when pressed.
2. Roll into 1" balls — a good task to split across the whole patrol.
3. Chill in the cooler 20-30 minutes to firm up, if time allows. Fine to
   eat right away otherwise.

## Real Food Check

- **Protein:** sunflower seed butter, oats
- **Processed:** mini chocolate chips — a flavor accent in a small amount,
  not the point of the snack; everything else is whole or minimally
  processed

## Allergy & Diet Notes

- **Nut-free:** check both the sunflower seed butter and chocolate chip
  brands for shared-facility warnings.
- **Egg-free:** yes.
- **Vegan:** yes, if made with maple syrup instead of honey.

## Scaling Notes

- **Patrol of 4:** 1 cup oats, scale the rest proportionally.
- **Patrol of 8:** 3 cups oats — mix in a bigger bowl, no other changes
  needed.

## Advancement Tie-In

- **Cooking merit badge:** satisfies the snack requirement for camp
  cooking — a good talking point for tying nutrition planning to an actual
  activity (a hike) rather than just a between-meal treat.
