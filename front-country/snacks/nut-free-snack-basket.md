# Nut-Free Snack Basket

*Any meal · Snack · Vegetarian (dairy-free option noted) · Nut-Free: Yes ·
Egg-Free: Yes · No-Cook · Serves 6 · Prep 10 min · Cook: none*

Two no-cook snack options, both built to be safe by default for a scout
with a nut allergy rather than requiring a special substitution — a crunchy
trail mix and a fruit-and-cheese plate. Lay both out and let the patrol
graze between activities.

## Ingredients (serves 6)

**Sunflower seed trail mix:**
- 2 cups roasted, salted sunflower seeds (shelled)
- 1 cup dried cranberries or raisins
- 1 cup pretzel pieces
- 1 cup nut-free chocolate chips or M&M's (M&M's are made in a facility
  that also processes peanuts — check current packaging if that matters
  for the specific allergy)

**Fruit & cheese plate:**
- 2 apples, sliced
- 1 lb grapes
- 8 oz cheese cubes or string cheese
- 1 sleeve crackers (check label for a nut-free brand)

## Equipment

- Mixing bowl (for trail mix) or just combine in a bag
- Cutting board and knife (for apples)

## Instructions

### Advance Prep (at home)

- Mix the trail mix at home and portion into a large bag or several small
  ones for hikes — no assembly needed at camp.
- Cube the cheese ahead if using block cheese instead of string cheese.

### Onsite (at camp)

1. **Trail mix:** if not already mixed at home, combine sunflower seeds,
   dried fruit, pretzels, and chocolate in a large bowl or gallon bag.
   Shake to mix.
2. **Fruit & cheese plate:** slice apples just before serving (toss with a
   little lemon juice or water if prepping more than 30 minutes ahead, to
   slow browning). Arrange with grapes, cheese, and crackers.

## Real Food Check

- **Protein:** sunflower seeds, cheese
- **Fruit:** apples, grapes, dried cranberries/raisins
- **Processed:** trail mix leans processed (chocolate/candy, pretzels);
  the fruit-and-cheese plate is straightforwardly whole-food

## Allergy & Diet Notes

- **Nut-free:** built to be nut-free from the ingredient list itself, not
  by substitution — read chocolate/candy and cracker labels for shared-
  facility warnings regardless, since this changes by brand and by year.
- **Egg-free:** yes.
- **Dairy-free option:** skip the cheese, add extra fruit or pretzels.
