# Dutch Oven Enchilada Bake — Vegetarian Base + Chicken Boost

*Dinner · Main · Vegetarian base · Nut-Free: Yes · Egg-Free: Yes ·
Dutch Oven · Serves 6 (base) + boost scaled to meat-eaters · Prep 15 min ·
Cook 30-35 min*

A layered casserole-style enchilada bake — torn tortillas instead of
individually rolled ones, so it's one shared dish, not thirty small ones.
Chicken is warmed separately and spooned onto individual portions.

## Ingredients

**Base (serves 6):**
- 12 corn or flour tortillas, torn or quartered
- 2 (10 oz) cans red or green enchilada sauce
- 1 (15 oz) can black beans, drained
- 1 cup corn, canned and drained (shelf-stable, no cooler space needed)
- 1/2 onion, diced
- 2.5 cups shredded Mexican cheese blend
- Optional: sliced olives, cilantro, sour cream, for serving

**Meat boost (sized to meat-eating scouts):**
- 2 cups cooked, shredded chicken (rotisserie or canned, covers up to 5-6
  meat-eating scouts)
- A splash of enchilada sauce, for warming the chicken

## Equipment

- 12" dutch oven + lid
- Charcoal chimney, ~24-26 coals
- Small pot (for warming chicken)
- Lid lifter, leather gloves

## Instructions

### Advance Prep (at home)

- Dice the onion ahead and cooler-pack — holds up fine for a day or two.
- Canned chicken needs no cooler space at all; if using rotisserie chicken
  instead, shred it at home and cooler-pack.

### Onsite (at camp)

1. Light ~24-26 coals. Oil the dutch oven.
2. Layer: a splash of enchilada sauce on the bottom, then torn tortillas,
   black beans, corn, onion, and cheese. Repeat the layers 2-3 times,
   finishing with cheese on top.
3. Cover. Set on 8 coals underneath, 16-17 coals on the lid. Bake 30-35
   minutes, rotating the oven and lid a quarter turn every 10 minutes,
   until bubbly and the cheese is melted through.
4. While it bakes, warm the shredded chicken in a small pot with a splash
   of enchilada sauce on a camp stove burner. Keep it in its own pot.
5. **To serve:** scoop portions from the dutch oven first — this is the
   vegetarian-safe dish. Meat-eating scouts top their own portion with
   warmed chicken from its own pot.

## Real Food Check

- **Protein (base):** black beans, cheese; + chicken (boost)
- **Vegetables:** corn, onion
- **Processed:** canned enchilada sauce — a minimally processed pantry

## Scaling Notes

- **Patrol of 4:** 8 tortillas, scale sauce/beans/cheese to 2/3; chicken
  sized to however many of the 4 eat meat.
- **Patrol of 8:** 16 tortillas, 1.3x sauce/beans/cheese, use a 14" oven if
  available; chicken sized per actual meat-eater headcount.
