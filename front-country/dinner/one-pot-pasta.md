# One-Pot Creamy White Bean Pasta — Vegetarian Base + Sausage Boost

*Dinner · Main · Vegetarian base · Nut-Free: Yes · Egg-Free: Yes ·
2-Burner Stove · Serves 6 (base) + boost scaled to meat-eaters · Prep 10
min · Cook 20 min*

Rich, garlicky pasta with white beans and sun-dried tomatoes is the shared
base — genuinely good on its own, not a diet dish. Meat-eating scouts stir
in browned Italian sausage from a separate skillet at serving time.

## Ingredients

**Base (serves 6):**
- 1 lb rotini or penne pasta
- 2 (15 oz) cans cannellini (white) beans, drained
- 1 (8 oz) jar sun-dried tomatoes in oil, chopped (reserve 2 tbsp oil)
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 cups fresh spinach (holds up fine in a cooler for a day or two —
  no need for frozen)
- 1/2 cup grated parmesan
- 1/2 cup half-and-half or cream (optional — reserved pasta water works too)
- Red pepper flakes, salt, pepper

**Meat boost (sized to meat-eating scouts):**
- 3/4 lb Italian sausage, casing removed (covers up to 5 meat-eating scouts)

## Equipment

- 2-burner stove
- Large pot (for pasta and base sauce), small skillet (for sausage)
- Colander, wooden spoon, and a second spoon dedicated to the sausage

## Instructions

### Advance Prep (at home)

- Mince the garlic ahead and cooler-pack in a small container.
- Decant red pepper flakes, salt, and pepper into one small spice
  container.
- The sun-dried tomato oil is already portioned inside its jar — no extra
  decanting needed there.

### Onsite (at camp)

1. Fill the large pot with salted water, boil on one burner. Cook pasta
   per package instructions. Reserve 1/2 cup pasta water before draining.
2. On the other burner, brown the sausage in the small skillet, breaking
   it up. Set aside in its own bowl — don't add it to the base yet.
3. In the now-empty large pot (or a second skillet), heat the reserved
   sun-dried tomato oil, sauté garlic 1 minute, add sun-dried tomatoes and
   white beans, cook 3-4 minutes.
4. Add spinach, stir until wilted. Stir in parmesan and cream (or reserved
   pasta water) until saucy. Combine with drained pasta. Season with red
   pepper flakes, salt, pepper.
5. **To serve:** portion the pasta base into bowls first — this is the
   vegetarian-safe dish, nothing else needs to happen to it. Meat-eating
   scouts then spoon sausage from its own bowl into their individual pasta
   bowls.

## Real Food Check

- **Protein (base):** cannellini beans, parmesan; + Italian sausage (boost)
- **Vegetables:** spinach, garlic, sun-dried tomatoes
- **Processed:** sun-dried tomatoes are a minimally processed pantry item,
  not ultra-processed; pasta is a refined grain — no need to add bread or
  more pasta on the side, the beans and greens are already carrying the
  meal

## Allergy & Diet Notes

- **Nut-free:** yes.
- **Egg-free:** yes (check the specific pasta brand's label).
- **Vegan option for the base:** skip parmesan and cream, use extra
  reserved pasta water and a drizzle of olive oil instead.

## Scaling Notes

- **Patrol of 4:** base at 2/3 quantities; sausage sized to however many
  of the 4 eat meat.
- **Patrol of 8:** base at 1.5x quantities, cook the sauce in two batches
  if the pan is too small; sausage still sized per actual meat-eater
  headcount.
