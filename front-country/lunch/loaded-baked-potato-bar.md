# Loaded Baked Potato Bar — Vegetarian Base + Bacon Boost

*Lunch · Main · Vegetarian base · Nut-Free: Yes · Egg-Free: Yes ·
Foil Pack/Coals · Serves 6 (base) + boost scaled to meat-eaters ·
Prep 10 min · Cook 45-60 min*

Foil-wrapped potatoes baked right in the coals, split open and loaded up
buffet-style. Good for a morning where the patrol is out on an activity —
start the potatoes in the coals before you leave, they hold heat well.

## Ingredients

**Base (serves 6):**
- 6 russet potatoes
- Oil, salt
- 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
- 1/2 cup sour cream
- 2 green onions, sliced
- 1 (15 oz) can black beans, drained (or steamed broccoli), for extra
  protein/fiber

**Meat boost (sized to meat-eating scouts):**
- 6 slices bacon, cooked and crumbled (covers up to 5-6 meat-eating scouts)

## Equipment

- Fire or coal bed
- Heavy-duty aluminum foil
- Tongs, a long skewer or knife (to test doneness)

## Instructions

### Advance Prep (at home)

- Decant the oil and salt into small travel containers — only a couple
  tablespoons of oil and a pinch of salt per potato is needed.
- Slice the green onions ahead and cooler-pack — they hold up fine for a
  day or two.

### Onsite (at camp)

1. Rub each potato with oil, sprinkle with salt, wrap individually in
   foil.
2. Nestle into the coals (or set on a grate close above them). Cook 45-60
   minutes, turning every 15 minutes, until a skewer slides through easily.
3. While potatoes cook, brown the bacon in a small skillet on a camp
   stove, drain, keep in its own bowl.
4. Split each potato open, fluff the inside with a fork. Lay out cheese,
   sour cream, green onion, and black beans as a topping bar.
5. **To serve:** the vegetarian scout builds their potato first from the
   shared toppings. Meat-eating scouts add bacon from its own bowl to
   their own potato.

## Scaling Notes

- **Patrol of 4:** 4 potatoes; bacon sized to however many of the 4 eat
  meat.
- **Patrol of 8:** 8 potatoes — start them earlier or bank more coals, a
  crowded coal bed cooks slower per potato.
