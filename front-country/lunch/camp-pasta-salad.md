# Camp Pasta Salad

*Lunch · Main · Vegetarian base, meat add-in optional · Nut-Free: Yes ·
Egg-Free: Yes · 2-Burner Stove · Serves 6 · Prep 15 min (plus cooling) ·
Cook 10 min*

A cold lunch for a hot, busy day — cook the pasta once in the morning, let
it chill in the cooler, serve at lunch with no reheating needed. Chickpeas
carry the protein so it's filling without meat.

## Ingredients (serves 6)

- 1 lb rotini or bowtie pasta
- 1 cup Italian dressing (oil-and-vinegar based, not mayo — travels and
  keeps better in a cooler)
- 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1 cucumber, diced
- 1/2 red onion, diced
- 1 cup mozzarella pearls or diced cheese
- 1 (15 oz) can chickpeas, drained
- Italian seasoning or fresh basil, salt, pepper
- Optional: 6 oz sliced pepperoni or salami, for meat-eating scouts

- Large pot, colander
- Large mixing bowl

## Instructions

### Advance Prep (at home)

- Pour the 1 cup of Italian dressing needed into a smaller travel bottle
  rather than packing the full store bottle.
- Decant Italian seasoning into a small container.
- Dice the cucumber and red onion ahead — both hold up fine for a day or
  two in the cooler; halve the cherry tomatoes on site so they don't turn
  watery.

### Onsite (at camp)

1. Boil pasta per package instructions. Drain and rinse under cool water
   (or spread on a tray in the cooler) to stop the cooking and cool it
   down for a cold salad.
2. Toss the cooled pasta with dressing, tomatoes, cucumber, onion, cheese,
   chickpeas, and seasoning. Chill in the cooler 20-30 minutes if time
   allows — it's fine to eat right away too, just less cold.
3. Serve. Meat-eating scouts add pepperoni or salami to their own bowl.

## Real Food Check

- **Protein:** chickpeas, mozzarella
- **Vegetables:** cherry tomatoes, cucumber, red onion
- **Processed:** bottled dressing — swap in a homemade oil-and-vinegar mix
  for a fully whole-food version

## Allergy & Diet Notes

- **Nut-free:** yes.
- **Egg-free:** yes (using an oil-and-vinegar dressing, not a mayo-based
  one, keeps this reliably egg-free — mayo contains egg).

## Scaling Notes

- **Patrol of 4:** 2/3 lb pasta, scale produce proportionally.
- **Patrol of 8:** 1.5 lb pasta — boil in two batches if the pot is too
  small.
