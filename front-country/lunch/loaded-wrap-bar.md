# Loaded Wrap Bar

*Lunch · Main · Meat & Vegetarian (one bar) · Nut-Free: Yes (check hummus
label) · Egg-Free: Yes · No-Cook · Serves 6 · Prep 10 min · Cook: none*

No stove needed — good for a lunch squeezed between morning and afternoon
activities, or a travel-day lunch. One spread of ingredients, everyone
builds their own wrap, meat and vegetarian scouts use the same table.

## Ingredients (serves 6, ~1.5 wraps each)

- 9 large tortillas or wraps
- 1 lb sliced deli turkey or ham
- 1 (10 oz) tub hummus or black bean spread (check label — most are
  nut-free, but confirm the brand)
- 1 cup shredded cheese
- 2 cups shredded lettuce
- 2 tomatoes, sliced
- Mustard and mayo packets
- Optional: sliced cucumber, bell pepper strips

## Shopping List

- [ ] Tortillas/wraps, 9
- [ ] Deli turkey or ham, 1 lb

- [ ] Tomatoes, 2
- [ ] Mustard, mayo packets

## Equipment

- Cutting board and knife (for tomatoes/cucumber)
- No stove needed — keep the cooler nearby, this is a cold lunch

## Instructions

### Advance Prep (at home)

- Wash and shred the lettuce, cooler-pack in a bag.
- Pre-slice the cucumber and bell pepper (they hold up fine for a day or
  two); tomatoes are better sliced on site so they don't go watery.
- Individual mustard/mayo packets already travel well — no decanting
  needed there.

### Onsite (at camp)

1. Lay out all ingredients on a table or tarp, tortillas at one end.
2. Meat wrap: tortilla, mustard/mayo, turkey or ham, cheese, lettuce,
   tomato — roll and fold in the ends.
3. Vegetarian wrap: tortilla, hummus, cheese, lettuce, tomato, cucumber/
   pepper strips — roll and fold in the ends.
4. Both wraps use the same tortillas, cheese, and veggies — only the
   protein spread changes, so there's no separate shopping or cooking for
   either option.

## Real Food Check

- **Protein:** deli turkey/ham, hummus, cheese
- **Vegetables:** lettuce, tomato (+ optional cucumber, bell pepper)
- **Processed:** deli meat is a processed product; everything else is
  minimally processed

## Allergy & Diet Notes

- **Nut-free:** yes as written, but hummus brands vary — read the label
  ("may contain tree nuts" warnings show up on some brands processed in
  shared facilities).
- **Egg-free:** yes, mayo packets are the only egg-adjacent item and are
  easy to skip or swap for mustard-only.

## Cost Estimate

~$3.85/scout for 6: tortillas ~$3.50, deli meat ~$9.50, hummus ~$4.50,
cheese ~$1.90, lettuce ~$1.40, tomatoes ~$2.30. Boston-area pricing — see
[OVERVIEW.md](../OVERVIEW.md#pricing-basis) for sourcing.

## Scaling Notes

- **Patrol of 4:** 6 tortillas, 2/3 lb deli meat, smaller hummus tub.
- **Patrol of 8:** 12 tortillas, 1.3 lb deli meat, larger hummus tub or two
  tubs.
